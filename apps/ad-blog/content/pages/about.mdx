ToolProof publishes practical guides to AI and everyday tech tools: how to use a new feature, how to fix an error that stops you, and how to choose between tools that look alike.

Most "news" about a new AI feature tells you that it launched. We focus on what comes next: where the setting actually is, what the free plan really includes, what to do when it doesn't work, and whether it is worth switching.

## What we cover

- **AI tools**: ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, and the AI features built into the apps you already use
- **Apps and software**: browsers, operating systems, and productivity apps, especially right after a big update changes how they work

## How we check our guides

- **We run it ourselves where we can.** If a tool, command, or website can be tested directly, we test it on a current version and write down exactly what happened, including the steps that did not work.
- **We cite primary sources.** Feature details, plan limits, and prices come from official documentation, release notes, and pricing pages, and we link to them so you can check.
- **We say what we could not test.** Some features are limited to a paid plan, a region, or a specific device. When a step comes from documentation rather than our own test, the article says so.
- **We update instead of guessing.** AI tools change quickly. When a feature, price, or menu changes, we update the guide and its "Updated" date.
