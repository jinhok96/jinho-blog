The best way to reach ToolProof is by email: <ContactEmail />

## What to write to us about

- **Corrections**: a step that no longer works, a wrong price or limit, or anything else that is inaccurate. Please include the page link and, if you can, the version or plan you are using.
- **Outdated guides**: a menu, setting, or feature that has changed since we published.
- **Topic suggestions**: a problem with an AI or tech tool you could not find a clear answer to.

We read every message and usually reply within three business days. We do not offer one-on-one technical support, and we do not accept paid placements or sponsored reviews.
