This Privacy Policy explains what information ToolProof ("we", "us") handles when you visit this website, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

- **We do not run accounts or comment forms.** You can read every page without giving us any personal information.
- **Email.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to fix the issue you reported, and we do not sell or share it.
- **Analytics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which guides are useful. It does not use cookies or local storage, does not track you across other websites, and does not build a profile of you. It records the page you visited, the referring page, and your browser, device type, and country. See [Cloudflare's Web Analytics privacy information](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/).
- **Server logs.** Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

## Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.

- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google in [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Ads may still appear, but they will be less relevant.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalized ads are shown.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California or another US state with a consumer privacy law, or the EEA and UK), you may have the right to know what personal information is processed about you, to ask for it to be deleted or corrected, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. To make a request, email us at the address below. You can also use the opt-out tools listed above at any time.

## Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Links to other websites

Our guides link to official documentation and other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.
