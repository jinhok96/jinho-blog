As of November 16, 2026, a home PC still gets Microsoft security fixes on Windows 11 version 25H2 (until October 12, 2027) or 26H2 (until October 10, 2028), on Windows 10 only if it is enrolled in Extended Security Updates (until October 12, 2027), and in Office 2024 (until October 9, 2029) or a Microsoft 365 subscription. The end of support date has already passed for Windows 11 Home and Pro version 24H2 and for Office 2021, both on October 13, 2026, and for Office 2016, Office 2019 and Windows 10 without ESU on October 14, 2025.

We checked every date on this page against Microsoft's own lifecycle pages, Windows release information and the November 10, 2026 update notes on November 16, 2026. We left out the servers, cloud services and developer tools that fill most end of support lists, so only versions you would find on a family PC are here. The November notes also show what happened after each deadline: Windows 11 Home and Pro version 24H2 was left out of the November security update, while Office 2021 was still listed. Two Microsoft pages disagree by one day on when version 26H2 ends, and we explain the difference in [How we checked](#how-we-checked).

## Microsoft end of support dates at a glance

Microsoft stops security updates for these home versions on the dates below, and the last column says what to do next.

| Windows 10 without ESU | October 14, 2025 | Ended | [Enroll in ESU](/posts/windows-10-esu-extended) or move to Windows 11 |
| Windows 10 with ESU | October 12, 2027 | Supported | Confirm your PC is enrolled |
| Windows 11 Home and Pro, 23H2 | November 11, 2025 | Ended | Update to a newer version |
| Windows 11 Home and Pro, 24H2 | October 13, 2026 | Ended | [Update to 26H2](/posts/windows-11-24h2-end-of-support) |
| Windows 11 Home and Pro, 25H2 | October 12, 2027 | Supported | Nothing urgent |

| Windows 11 Home and Pro, 26H2 | October 10, 2028 | Supported | Nothing to do |

Most of these dates fall on a Tuesday because Windows monthly security updates come out on the second Tuesday of each month, according to Microsoft's [Windows release cycle](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/deployment/update/release-cycle) page, and Microsoft's Office security notes for September, October and November 2026 are dated on those same Tuesdays.

## Which versions got the November 2026 security update?

The November 10, 2026 security update reached Windows 11 versions 25H2 and 26H2, Windows 10 PCs enrolled in ESU, Office 2024 and, despite its end of support, Office 2021, but it skipped Windows 11 Home and Pro version 24H2.

- **Windows 11.** The November update, [KB5132061](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/5132061), applies to "Windows 11 version 26H2, all editions," "version 25H2, all editions" and "Windows 11 Enterprise and Education, version 24H2." Home and Pro editions of 24H2 are not on that list, so a 24H2 home PC got no November fix.
- **Windows 10.** The November update for Windows 10, [KB5131096](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/5131096), lists "Windows 10 ESU" as the home edition it applies to. Microsoft followed it on November 13 with [KB5136493](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/5136493), a non-security fix for a Windows activation error that mainly affected organizations.
- **Office.** Microsoft's [Office security release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/officeupdates/microsoft365-apps-security-updates) for November 10 list "Office 2021 Retail: Version 2610 (Build 20527.20196)," the same build as Office 2024. Office 2019 and Office 2016 retail versions are not listed; only Office 2019 Volume Licensed, the version businesses buy in bulk, still appears.

![Table of who got the November 10, 2026 security update: Windows 11 Home and Pro 26H2 and 25H2 yes, listed in KB5132061; Windows 11 Home and Pro 24H2 no, because KB5132061 names only Enterprise and Education 24H2; Windows 10 22H2 with ESU yes, KB5131096 plus a November 13 non-security fix; Office 2024 yes, Version 2610 Build 20527.20196; Office 2021 retail yes, same build even though support ended October 13; Office 2019 and 2016 retail no, only Office 2019 Volume Licensed appears](./images/microsoft-end-of-support-dates/november-2026-security-update-check.webp)

Don't treat the Office 2021 build as a promise. Microsoft's [Office 2021 end of support page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/system-requirements/end-of-support-for-office-2021) still says "there will be no extension and no extended security updates." Our [Office 2021 guide](/posts/office-2021-end-of-support) shows the month-by-month record and how the retail version of Office 2019 dropped out right after its own deadline.

## How to check which version you have

Windows shows its edition and version in Settings, and Office shows its product name on the Account page.

To check Windows, per Microsoft's [device information guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/experience/find-information-about-your-windows-device):

1. Right-click **Start** and choose **Settings**.
2. Select **System > About**.
3. Under **Windows specifications**, read **Edition** (for example, Windows 11 Home) and **Version** (for example, 24H2).

To check Office, per Microsoft's [Office version guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/lifecycle/lc-account/about-office-what-version-of-office-am-i-using):

1. Open Word or Excel.
2. Select **Account**. If a document is already open, select **File > Account**.
3. Under **Product Information**, read the product name, such as Office Home & Student 2021 or Microsoft 365.

If the name says Microsoft 365, you have the subscription version, which follows the Windows rules in the [Microsoft 365 on Windows 10 section](#microsoft-365-apps-on-windows-10-get-fixes-until-october-10-2028) rather than a fixed end date. The [Office 2021 guide](/posts/office-2021-end-of-support) also covers the Mac steps.

## Windows 11 version 24H2 has ended, 25H2 and 26H2 have not

Each Windows 11 version gets 24 months of security updates on Home and Pro, so version 24H2 stopped on October 13, 2026, 25H2 stops on October 12, 2027 and 26H2 stops on October 10, 2028.

Leaving 24H2 takes one restart. Microsoft's [26H2 enablement package](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/5121794) applies to Home and Pro versions 24H2 and 25H2, and it moves a PC to 26H2 "with a single restart" through Windows Update. Our [Windows 11 24H2 guide](/posts/windows-11-24h2-end-of-support) walks through the update, what to do when Windows Update holds it back, and why 26H2 is a better pick than 25H2.

Two versions don't follow the usual pattern:

- **26H1** is for new PCs that started coming to market in early 2026. Microsoft's [Windows 11 release information](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/release-health/windows11-release-information) says it is "not offered as an in-place update from 24H2 or 25H2," and its updates run to March 14, 2028.
- **Windows 11 SE** stops at 24H2. Microsoft's [Windows 11 Home and Pro lifecycle page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/windows-11-home-and-pro) says 24H2 "was the last supported version" for SE.

New Windows features also arrive only on supported versions. The [customizable right-click menu](/posts/customize-right-click-menu-windows-11) began rolling out in the October 28, 2026 optional update, KB5127936, which the release information page lists for 25H2 and 26H2 but not 24H2. If you're on a supported version and want fewer AI features, our guide to [turning off AI in Windows 11](/posts/turn-off-ai-in-windows-11) covers each switch.

## Windows 10 stays patched until October 12, 2027 only with ESU

A Windows 10 home PC gets security updates only if it is enrolled in Microsoft's consumer Extended Security Updates (ESU) program, which now runs until October 12, 2027.

According to Microsoft's [ESU page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/extended-security-updates), checked November 16, 2026:

- You can enroll at no extra cost if you sync your PC settings to a Microsoft account, by redeeming 1,000 Microsoft Rewards points, or with a one-time $30 purchase.
- One enrollment covers up to 10 PCs signed in with the same Microsoft account.
- PCs that are already enrolled continue "automatically" through October 12, 2027, with no action needed.
- ESU covers security updates only. It adds no new features and no technical support.
- Enrollment options can differ by region, for example in the European Economic Area.

To see whether a PC is enrolled, open **Settings > Update & Security > Windows Update**. Our [Windows 10 ESU guide](/posts/windows-10-esu-extended) covers the enrollment screens and the errors people run into. Enrolling doesn't stop you from upgrading later. The ESU page says you can still move to Windows 11 if your PC meets the hardware requirements.

## Office 2016, 2019 and 2021 no longer get security fixes

Office 2016 and Office 2019 stopped getting security updates on October 14, 2025, and Office 2021 stopped on October 13, 2026, including the Mac versions of Office 2021.

The apps don't stop opening. Microsoft's Office 2021 page says the apps "may continue to function," but there will be no more security fixes, bug fixes or phone and chat support, so a security flaw found later stays unfixed. The supported choices are:

- **Office 2024**, the current one-time purchase for Windows and Mac. Microsoft's [Office 2024 lifecycle page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/office-2024) lists support until October 9, 2029.
- **Microsoft 365**, the subscription, which keeps updating as long as you pay and your PC runs a supported system.
- **Free Word, Excel and PowerPoint on the web.** Microsoft's [free web apps page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/free-office-online-for-the-web) offers them free in a browser after you sign up.

Our [Office 2021 guide](/posts/office-2021-end-of-support) compares what each choice costs over three years and shows how to install the last updates before you switch.

## Microsoft 365 apps on Windows 10 get fixes until October 10, 2028

Microsoft keeps sending security updates to Microsoft 365 apps such as Word, Excel and Outlook on Windows 10 until October 10, 2028, three years after Windows 10 itself lost support.

Microsoft's [Windows 10 and Microsoft 365 Apps page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-apps/end-of-support/windows-10-support) sets two limits:

- New features and Copilot support stop at Version 2608, and Windows 10 PCs "remain on Version 2608, receiving only security updates." Version 2608 was already listed as the Current Channel version in Microsoft's September 8, 2026 security notes, so Windows 10 PCs have likely reached that cutoff.
- The OneDrive desktop app keeps updating on Windows 10 version 22H2 until the same October 10, 2028 date.

Support is limited in the meantime. If a problem happens only on Windows 10, Microsoft's support team will ask you to move to Windows 11. These app updates also don't protect Windows itself, which still needs ESU.

## Publisher was removed from Microsoft 365 in October 2026

Microsoft 365 subscribers lost Publisher on or shortly after October 1, 2026, and support for one-time purchase versions ended on October 13, 2026, together with Office 2021.

Microsoft's [Publisher retirement page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/publisher/microsoft-publisher-will-no-longer-be-supported-after-october-2026) says subscribers can no longer open or edit Publisher files in Publisher. If a PC still has a one-time purchase copy, such as Publisher 2021, save your files in another format while it still opens them:

1. Open the Publisher file.
2. Select **File > Save as** and pick a folder.
3. Choose **PDF** as the file type and select **Save**.

To edit the content again, open the PDF in Word with **File > Open**. Microsoft warns that the layout may change, especially in files with many pictures. For envelopes, labels, calendars and business cards, Microsoft points to Word and PowerPoint instead. If no copy of Publisher opens your files anymore, our [test of free Publisher to PDF converters](/posts/convert-publisher-file-to-pdf) found that Zamzar kept the layout while LibreOffice repeated the text of newsletter columns.

## Other Microsoft dates that affect home users

Two more Microsoft changes with fixed dates reach home users even though they aren't end of support dates.

- **Microsoft 365 Family storage.** Family and Premium plans move from storage per person to one 2 TB pool shared by up to six people. Existing plans switch at the first renewal on or after May 2, 2027, according to Microsoft's [storage changes page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/onedrive/changes-to-microsoft-365-storage). Our [Microsoft 365 Family storage guide](/posts/microsoft-365-family-storage-2tb) shows who loses space.
- **Work email in Apple Mail.** Microsoft began turning off Exchange Web Services in Exchange Online in October 2026, and its [deprecation page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/exchange/clients-and-mobile-in-exchange-online/deprecation-of-ews-exchange-online) says the service will be "fully disabled" in April 2027. Our [Apple Mail Exchange guide](/posts/apple-mail-exchange-not-syncing) explains why that can stop work email from syncing on a Mac and what to use instead.

## What to do with the version you have

Pick the line that matches the version you found in Settings or on the Office Account page.

![Table of what to do by version, based on Microsoft support dates checked November 16, 2026: Windows 11 24H2 Home or Pro, install the free 26H2 update from Windows Update for fixes until October 10, 2028; Windows 11 25H2, nothing urgent, fixes until October 12, 2027; Windows 10, enroll in ESU for free with settings sync, 1,000 Rewards points or $30, until October 12, 2027; Office 2016, 2019 or 2021, move to Office 2024, Microsoft 365 or free Office on the web; Microsoft 365 on Windows 10, security fixes until October 10, 2028 but no new features after Version 2608; Publisher, save each file as a PDF and open it in Word to edit](./images/microsoft-end-of-support-dates/what-to-do-by-version.webp)

- **Windows 11 Home or Pro, 24H2:** install 26H2 now. It takes one restart and brings fixes until October 10, 2028. Skipping 25H2 avoids doing this again in October 2027.
- **Windows 11, 25H2:** nothing is urgent before October 12, 2027. Take 26H2 when Windows Update offers it.
- **Windows 10 on a PC that can run Windows 11:** upgrade, since ESU ends in October 2027 and Windows 11 versions keep going.
- **Windows 10 on a PC that can't run Windows 11:** enroll in ESU, ideally the free way through settings sync, and plan the PC's replacement before October 12, 2027.
- **Office 2016, 2019 or 2021 for occasional documents:** the free web versions of Word, Excel and PowerPoint handle everyday files at no cost.
- **Office 2016, 2019 or 2021 as a daily tool:** Office 2024 runs until October 2029 with no subscription, while Microsoft 365 keeps updating for as long as you pay. The [Office 2021 guide](/posts/office-2021-end-of-support) has the cost comparison.
- **Publisher files:** convert them to PDF now, while any copy of Publisher still opens them.

## Dates to remember

- **December 8, 2026.** The next monthly security update. It will show whether Office 2021 is still listed.
- **April 2027.** Exchange Web Services fully off.
- **May 2, 2027.** Existing Microsoft 365 Family and Premium plans start moving to shared storage at renewal.
- **October 12, 2027.** Windows 10 ESU and Windows 11 Home and Pro version 25H2 end.
- **March 14, 2028.** Windows 11 version 26H1 ends.
- **October 10, 2028.** Windows 11 Home and Pro version 26H2 ends, and so do security updates for Microsoft 365 apps and OneDrive on Windows 10.
- **October 9, 2029.** Office 2024 ends.

## How we checked

On November 16, 2026, we opened and saved copies of Microsoft's lifecycle pages for Windows 10 Home and Pro, Windows 11 Home and Pro, Office 2016, Office 2021 and Office 2024; the [Ending Support in 2025](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/end-of-support/end-of-support-2025), [2026](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/end-of-support/end-of-support-2026) and [2027](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/end-of-support/end-of-support-2027) lists; the Windows 10 and Windows 11 release information pages; the November 2026 update notes for Windows and Office; and the ESU, Microsoft 365, Publisher and storage pages linked above. We did not install anything on a Windows PC for this page. The step-by-step details come from our guides for each version, which we checked between October and November 2026.

Microsoft's pages don't always agree, so here is what we did where they differ:

- **Windows 11 26H2 end date.** The lifecycle page lists October 9, 2028, a Monday, while the Windows 11 release information page lists October 10, 2028 for Home and Pro, the second Tuesday, when monthly updates ship. We use October 10, 2028.
- **The 2027 list is incomplete for home users.** "Ending Support in 2027" includes Enterprise and Education 24H2 on October 12, 2027, but not Windows 11 Home and Pro 25H2 or Windows 10 consumer ESU, which end on the same day according to the version and ESU pages. Check the page for your exact version, not only the yearly list.
- **Office 2019.** The lifecycle address for Office 2019 returned "Page not found," so its October 14, 2025 date comes from the Ending Support in 2025 list.
