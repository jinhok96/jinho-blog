Microsoft ended support for Office 2021 on October 13, 2026, with no extension and no paid Extended Security Updates. Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook 2021 still open and save your files on Windows and Mac, but Microsoft no longer promises to fix security holes in them.

In the first month after the cutoff, fixes came anyway. Microsoft's November 10, 2026 security release notes list an Office 2021 build, and its security update data shows more than 170 vulnerabilities fixed for Office 2021 on Windows and Mac. Microsoft hasn't said how long that will last, and Office 2019's retail copies dropped off the same list right after their own end date. So install the latest update now, then plan a replacement: Microsoft's free web apps if you only edit now and then, Office Home 2024 ($179.99 once, supported until October 9, 2029) for one computer, or Microsoft 365 Family for three or more.

We checked Microsoft's end-of-support notice, its lifecycle pages, its monthly Office security release notes going back to 2021, its November 2026 security update data, the Office for Mac release notes and its current U.S. prices on November 13, 2026.

## Is Office 2021 still supported?

No: Microsoft's support for Office 2021 ended on October 13, 2026, and its [end-of-support notice](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/system-requirements/end-of-support-for-office-2021) says "there will be no extension and no extended security updates." Microsoft also ended phone and chat support and says most of its online help for Office 2021 will be retired.

The cutoff covers every one-time-purchase edition: Home and Student, Home and Business and Professional, including the Mac versions, according to the [Office 2021 lifecycle page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/office-2021). The business version, Office LTSC 2021, ended on the same day ([2026 end-of-support list](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/end-of-support/end-of-support-2026)). Microsoft 365 subscriptions are a different product and keep getting updates.

| If you use... | Supported until | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office Home and Student 2021, Home and Business 2021, Professional 2021 (Windows or Mac) | Ended October 13, 2026 | Update it now, then pick a replacement |
| Office LTSC 2021 at work or school | Ended October 13, 2026 | Ask your IT help desk; it's their call |
| Office 2016 or Office 2019 | Ended October 14, 2025 | Same choices as Office 2021 |
| Office Home 2024 or Home & Business 2024 | October 9, 2029 | Nothing |
| Microsoft 365 Personal, Family or Premium | As long as you subscribe | Nothing |
| Word, Excel and PowerPoint for the web (free) | Kept up to date by Microsoft | Nothing |

Dates come from Microsoft's lifecycle pages for [Office 2021](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/office-2021) and [Office 2024](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/office-2024) as of November 13, 2026. The apps don't stop working on the end date: Microsoft says they "may continue to function."

## Does Office 2021 still get security updates?

Office 2021 received security fixes in November 2026, the first month after its end of support, but Microsoft doesn't promise any more. Its [release notes for Office security updates](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/officeupdates/microsoft365-apps-security-updates) list "Office 2021 Retail: Version 2610 (Build 20527.20196)" and "Office LTSC 2021 Volume Licensed: Version 2108 (Build 14334.21048)" under November 10, 2026, the builds that contain that month's fixes. Microsoft's November 2026 security update data, published by its Security Response Center, agrees: it lists 172 vulnerabilities fixed in Office LTSC 2021 build 14334.21048 on Windows and 141 fixed in Office LTSC for Mac 2021 version 16.115.

That doesn't match Microsoft's end-of-support notice, which still says there will be no more security fixes, and Microsoft hasn't published anything explaining the difference. The history of the same release notes is the best guide to what may come next.

![Grid of the Office versions listed in Microsoft's monthly security release notes from September 2025 to November 2026: Office 2016 and 2019 retail are listed in September and October 2025 and missing every month after their October 2025 end date; Office 2019 volume licensed, Office 2021 retail, Office LTSC 2021 and Office 2024 are listed every month, including November 2026 after the Office 2021 end date](./images/office-2021-end-of-support/office-security-notes-by-month.webp)

- **Office 2016 and Office 2019 retail copies** were last listed on October 14, 2025, their end-of-support date, and haven't appeared since.
- **Office 2019 volume licensed copies**, the ones businesses buy, have been listed every month since, for 13 months. The page explains that "at Microsoft's sole discretion, we may choose to issue one or more updates to Office 2019" after its end date.
- **Office 2021** has no such note, but both its retail and volume licensed copies were still listed in November 2026.

So install the November fixes, and plan a replacement anyway. The next monthly security release is Tuesday, December 8, 2026; if Office 2021 drops off the list then or in any later month, the fixes have stopped.

## How to check which Office you have

Your Office version is shown on the account screen of any Office app, according to Microsoft's [version guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/lifecycle/lc-account/about-office-what-version-of-office-am-i-using).

- **Windows**: open Word or Excel and select **Account** (or **File > Account** if a document is open). Under **Product Information**, the product name tells you what you have, such as Office Home and Student 2021.
- **Mac**: open Word and choose **Word > About Word**. The window shows the version number and the license type.

If it says Microsoft 365, you're covered and can stop here. If it says Office 2021, 2019 or 2016, read on. If it's a work or school computer, ask your IT team first: Microsoft says managed copies follow your organization's own upgrade plan.

## How to install the last Office 2021 updates

Update Office 2021 now so it has the November 2026 fixes, whatever you decide to replace it with.

**On Windows**, these steps come from Microsoft's [guide to updating Office](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/lifecycle/officeinstall/how-to-update-microsoft-365-or-office-for-windows):

1. Open Word and create a new blank document.
2. Select **File > Account**.
3. Under **Product Information**, select **Update Options > Update Now**. If you don't see **Update Now**, select **Enable Updates** first.
4. Close the "You're up to date!" or "Updates were installed" window when it appears.

If there's no **Update Options** button and only **About**, Microsoft says you have a volume license copy or your company manages updates, so check with your help desk.

**On a Mac**, open Word and choose **Help > Check for Updates**, which opens Microsoft AutoUpdate, according to Microsoft's [Office for Mac release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/officeupdates/release-notes-office-for-mac). The same page says you need macOS 14 Sonoma or later to receive Office updates; on an older macOS, the apps keep working but get no updates at all, including security fixes.

### If Office 2021 for Mac stopped working in October

An Office for Mac update in October 2026 switched off Office 2021 for people who already had it, and Microsoft released the fix in later updates. The release notes for version 16.114.3 (October 27) and 16.114.4 (November 4) both say: "A previous update inadvertently disabled Office 2021 for existing users. This update resolves the issue and fully restores functionality for affected customers." If Word or Excel 2021 on your Mac suddenly stopped letting you edit or asked for Microsoft 365, use **Help > Check for Updates** to install version 16.114.4 or later before you reinstall anything or buy a subscription.

## What to replace Office 2021 with

The cheapest replacement depends on how many computers need desktop Office: free if browser apps are enough, Office Home 2024 for one computer, Microsoft 365 Family for three or more.

| [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/download/) 26.8.3 | Free | Desktop word processor, spreadsheet and slides for Windows and Mac |

Prices are from Microsoft's [plan comparison page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/buy/compare-all-microsoft-365-products) and its Office 2024 product pages on November 13, 2026. Microsoft 365 Personal was $69.99 for the first year under an offer that day, renewing at $99.99. One-time purchases have "no upgrade options," the comparison page says, so the next version after Office 2024 costs full price.

## Which option should you choose?

Start with the free web apps if you open Word or Excel a few times a month. Upload one of your real files and see whether it works for you; if it does, you don't need a paid replacement at all.

- **One computer, desktop apps wanted**: Office Home 2024. At $179.99 once, it works out to about $60 a year until October 2029, against $99.99 a year for Microsoft 365 Personal. Skip it if you rely on desktop Outlook, which needs the $249.99 Home & Business edition or a subscription.
- **Two computers**: close call. Two Office Home 2024 licenses cost $359.98 against $389.97 for three years of Microsoft 365 Family, so Family's Outlook, newer versions and storage (which [shrinks for big families](/posts/microsoft-365-family-storage-2tb) when existing plans renew after May 2027) likely decide it.
- **Three or more computers**: Microsoft 365 Family. Three Office Home 2024 licenses cost $539.97, while Family costs $389.97 over three years and covers up to six people.
- **You read your email in Outlook 2021**: replace that first. Email attachments and messages from other people are where an unpatched Office is most exposed; until you switch, don't open Word or Excel files you weren't expecting, especially ones that ask you to enable editing or content.

If you use Office 2021 only for your own documents, never open files from others and read email somewhere else, keeping it updated for a while longer is a reasonable choice, as long as you know Microsoft can stop the fixes in any month without notice.

## Office dates to remember

- **December 8, 2026**: the next monthly security release ([Patch Tuesday](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/deployment/update/release-cycle)), the first chance to see whether Office 2021 is still on Microsoft's list.
- **October 9, 2029**: Office 2024 reaches its end of support, according to its [lifecycle page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/office-2024).

If the same family computer still runs Windows 10, check that it's enrolled in [Extended Security Updates through October 2027](/posts/windows-10-esu-extended). A Windows 11 PC on version 24H2 has its own cutoff: [Home and Pro editions stopped getting updates on October 13](/posts/windows-11-24h2-end-of-support), the same day as Office 2021. If you read Microsoft 365 work email on a Mac, a separate change applies: [Microsoft is switching off the connection that Apple Mail and legacy Outlook for Mac use](/posts/apple-mail-exchange-not-syncing).
