On an iPhone with iOS 27, there is no longer one switch that turns off Apple Intelligence. You turn it off one feature at a time in the **Settings** app: switch Siri to **Siri Classic** under **Settings > Siri**, then turn off the summaries under **Settings > Notifications** and **Settings > Apps > Messages** and **Mail**. To keep Siri AI off for good, use **Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > Siri** and allow only **Siri (Classic Version)**.

We compared Apple's iPhone User Guide for iOS 27 with the iOS 26 edition on October 23, 2026. The iOS 26 guide still has a **Turn off Apple Intelligence** section with one button. The iOS 27 guide replaces it with a list of nine separate settings, and some Apple Intelligence tools, such as Writing Tools and Image Playground, have no off switch in that list at all. We didn't have an iPhone running iOS 27, so the menu names below come from Apple's own guides, not our screen.

![Comparison of turning off Apple Intelligence in iOS 26 and iOS 27: iOS 26 has one switch under Settings, Apple Intelligence and Siri, which you turn on yourself; iOS 27 has no single switch, turns on automatically after the update, and is turned off one feature at a time](./images/turn-off-apple-intelligence-iphone/apple-intelligence-ios-26-vs-ios-27.webp)

| Feature | Where to turn it off (iOS 27) |
| --- | --- |
| Siri AI | **Settings > Siri > Turn Off Siri**, then **Turn on Siri > Siri Classic** |
| Notification summaries | **Settings > Notifications > Summarize Notifications** |
| Priority notifications | **Settings > Notifications > Prioritize Notifications** |
| Message summaries | **Settings > Apps > Messages > Summarize Messages** |
| Email summaries | **Settings > Apps > Mail > Summarize Message Previews** |
| ChatGPT | **Settings > Siri > ChatGPT > Use ChatGPT** |
| All of Siri AI, for good | **Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > Siri** |

## Can you turn off Apple Intelligence completely in iOS 27?

No, iOS 27 has no single switch that turns off all of Apple Intelligence on an iPhone. Apple says the new Apple Intelligence is "integrated deeply into the OS" and is "automatically available on eligible devices" when you update to iOS 27 ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)). Apple's iOS 27 guide to [turning off and restricting Apple Intelligence features](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-restrict-access-apple-intelligence-iph3fed3f2c3/ios) lists separate settings instead: Siri AI, message summaries, email summaries, notification summaries, priority notifications, personalized Smart Replies in Mail, voicemail suggestions, Call Context cards and Journal writing prompts.

That's a change from iOS 26. Apple's [iOS 26 guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/intro-to-apple-intelligence-iphc28624b81/26/ios/26) still says: "Go to the Settings app, tap Apple Intelligence & Siri, then tap the button next to Apple Intelligence." If your iPhone is still on iOS 26, that one button is the fastest way to turn it all off. You can check your version under **Settings > General > About**.

Apple Intelligence runs only on the iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 models and later, including iPhone Air and iPhone Duo. Older iPhones don't have these features, so there is nothing to turn off.

## How to turn off Siri AI and go back to Siri Classic

Turning off Siri, then turning it back on with **Siri Classic**, gives you the older Siri without its AI abilities, according to Apple's iOS 27 guide.

1. Open the **Settings** app and tap **Siri**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Turn Off Siri**, then tap **Turn Off Siri** again to confirm.
3. To keep using Siri without AI, tap **Turn on Siri**, then tap **Siri Classic**.

If you'd rather not have Siri at all, stop after step 2.

Siri AI is still labeled a beta. Apple says it is available in English to start and won't initially be available in the European Union on iOS, so if you're in the EU or use Siri in another language, you may only have Siri Classic already. iOS 27.2, released October 26, 2026, added French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish and let Siri AI recall your past conversations; our [breakdown of iOS 27.2's Siri changes](/posts/ios-27-2-siri-ai-memory) covers the new Excluded Apps setting that limits what it reads.

## How to turn off notification summaries

Notification summaries turn off under **Settings > Notifications > Summarize Notifications**, according to Apple's guide to [summarizing notifications](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/summarize-notifications-reduce-interruptions-iph1fbe7d2b9/ios).

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Notifications**.
2. Tap **Summarize Notifications**.
3. Turn off **Summarize Notifications**.

To keep summaries for some apps and not others, leave the switch on and tap **Choose Notifications to Summarize**, then turn off the apps you don't want summarized.

Priority notifications, which move the notifications Apple Intelligence thinks matter most to the top of the stack, are a separate switch: **Settings > Notifications > Prioritize Notifications**. When a priority notification is on your Lock Screen, you can also swipe left on it, tap **Options**, then tap **Turn Off Prioritization**.

## How to turn off summaries in Messages and Mail

Messages and Mail each have their own summary switch under **Settings > Apps**, separate from notification summaries.

- **Messages:** go to **Settings > Apps > Messages** and turn off **Summarize Messages**.
- **Mail:** go to **Settings > Apps > Mail** and turn off **Summarize Message Previews**.

While you're in the Mail settings, you can also turn off **Personalize Smart Replies**, the suggested replies Apple Intelligence writes in your own style.

## How to turn off other Apple Intelligence suggestions

Three smaller features have their own switches in apps you might not think to check, according to Apple's iOS 27 guide.

- **Voicemail suggestions:** **Settings > Apps > Phone**, then turn off **Suggestions in Voicemail**.
- **Call Context cards:** **Settings > Apps > Phone > Apple Intelligence & Siri**, then tap **Show When Calling** to change it. These are the cards that show related information during a call.
- **Journal writing prompts:** **Settings > Apps > Journal**, then turn off **Get Writing Prompts**.

## How to turn off ChatGPT in Siri

ChatGPT is a separate extension, and it turns off under **Settings > Siri > ChatGPT** by turning off **Use ChatGPT**, according to Apple's guide to [ChatGPT on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-on-chatgpt-iph00fd3c8c2/ios). If you use Siri AI, the path is **Settings > Siri > Extensions > ChatGPT**, then turn off **Use Extension**.

Apple says that when you use the extension without signing in, your request and a few details such as time zone, country, device type and language are sent to ChatGPT, and your IP address is hidden. If you're signed in to a ChatGPT account, OpenAI's own data policies apply.

## How to block Siri AI for good with Screen Time

Screen Time is the way to keep Siri AI off so it can't be turned back on from the Siri settings, for example on a child's iPhone. Apple's [Screen Time guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/block-features-or-content-with-screen-time-iph3ff83f3b1/ios) gives these steps:

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Screen Time**.
2. Tap **Content & Privacy Restrictions**, then tap **Siri**.
3. Choose one:
   - **Siri (Classic Version)** to allow Siri without AI.
   - **Don't Allow Siri** to block Siri completely.
   - **Siri AI (Beta)** to allow it, then set **Allow** or **Block** for single abilities such as **Image Creation**, **Sensitive Topics** or **Explicit Language**.

Apple notes that the options shown here can vary depending on your Siri version. To stop anyone else from changing them back, Apple's guide to [Screen Time passcodes](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-change-remove-a-screen-time-passcode-iph272b4c4bd/ios) has you go to **Settings > Screen Time > Manage Screen Time > Lock Screen Time Settings** and enter a four-digit passcode.

In iOS 26, the same Screen Time menu was called **Intelligence & Siri** and had separate **Writing Tools**, **Image Creation** and **Intelligence Extensions** blocks, according to Apple's [iOS 26 Screen Time guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/block-access-to-apple-intelligence-features-iph3fed3f2c3/26/ios/26). The iOS 27 guide doesn't list a Writing Tools block.

## What you can't turn off

Writing Tools, the photo editing tools and Image Playground have no off switch in Apple's iOS 27 guide. The full list of settings Apple gives doesn't include Writing Tools (including automatic proofreading), **Clean Up**, **Extend** and **Reframe** in Photos, Image Playground, Genmoji or Visual Intelligence. Most of these only do something when you tap them, so leaving them alone is the practical way to not use them.

![Three groups of Apple Intelligence features on iOS 27: features with their own off switch, such as Siri AI, summaries, priority notifications, Smart Replies and the ChatGPT extension; features that can be blocked in Screen Time, such as Siri AI and its Image Creation ability; and features with no off switch in Apple's guide, such as Writing Tools, Clean Up, Image Playground, Genmoji and Visual Intelligence](./images/turn-off-apple-intelligence-iphone/apple-intelligence-what-you-can-turn-off.webp)

Turning features off also isn't a way to win back storage. Apple says Apple Intelligence uses up to 8 GB on most supported iPhones and up to 14 GB on the iPhone 17 Pro models, iPhone Air and iPhone Duo ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115)). Apple's guides don't say that any of the switches above removes those files, so plan on that space staying in use.

## How to see what Apple Intelligence sent to Apple's servers

The **Apple Intelligence Report** shows the requests your iPhone sent to Private Cloud Compute, Apple's servers for tasks too big to run on the phone. Apple's [Apple Intelligence and privacy guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/apple-intelligence-and-privacy-iphe3f499e0e/ios) gives the path:

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Privacy & Security**.
2. Tap **Apple Intelligence Report**, then choose **last 15 minutes** or **last 7 days**.
3. Tap **Export Activity** to save the report as a file.

If you choose off in step 2, the report stops recording. Apple says data processed in Private Cloud Compute isn't stored or made accessible to Apple, and many requests run entirely on the iPhone and never reach the servers.

## How to turn off Apple Intelligence on iPad and Mac

iPad and Mac follow the same feature-by-feature approach as iPhone, according to Apple's iPadOS 27 and macOS 27 guides.

- **iPad (iPadOS 27):** the [iPad guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/turn-restrict-access-apple-intelligence-ipad3b7f1217/ipados) uses the same **Settings** paths as iPhone, from **Settings > Siri** to **Settings > Apps > Mail**. It covers the iPad mini (A17 Pro) and iPad models with M1 or later.
- **Mac (macOS 27):** the [Mac guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/turn-restrict-access-apple-intelligence-mchlb2e44f94/mac) turns off Siri AI and notification summaries in **System Settings**, but message and email summaries and Smart Replies are inside each app: **Messages > Settings**, **Mail > Settings > Viewing** and **Mail > Settings > Composing**. The Mac list doesn't include priority notifications.

## Which settings should you turn off?

- **If you only want the old Siri back:** switch to **Siri Classic** under **Settings > Siri**. Nothing else changes.
- **If summaries have gotten something wrong:** turn off **Summarize Notifications**, then the Messages and Mail summary switches. These three are where Apple Intelligence rewrites what other people sent you.
- **If you're setting up a child's iPhone:** use Screen Time to allow only **Siri (Classic Version)**, set a Screen Time passcode, then turn off the summaries.
- **If you want AI off everywhere, not just on your iPhone:** our guide to [turning off AI in Chrome, Gmail, Word and more](/posts/turn-off-ai-features) shows how far each product lets you go, with full guides for [Gemini in Gmail](/posts/turn-off-gemini-in-gmail), [Copilot in Word](/posts/turn-off-copilot-in-word) and [AI in Chrome](/posts/turn-off-ai-in-chrome).

## FAQ

### Does turning off Apple Intelligence affect the Siri settlement?

No. The $250 million settlement over Siri features Apple advertised but didn't ship pays people who bought an eligible iPhone in the U.S. between June 2024 and March 2025, whatever settings they use now. Our [Apple Siri settlement guide](/posts/apple-siri-settlement-claim) covers who can claim and the December 21, 2026 deadline.

### How do I tell if an image was made with Apple Intelligence?

There's no built-in checker on iPhone yet. Apple says support for the SynthID watermark, which lets you identify images generated or edited with AI, will arrive "in a software update later this year" ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/open-up-to-new-possibilities-with-iphone-duo/)). Our guide on [how to check if an image is AI-generated](/posts/check-if-image-is-ai-generated) covers the free tools you can use now.
