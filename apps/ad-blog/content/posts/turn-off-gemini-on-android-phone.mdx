To turn off Gemini on an Android phone completely, open **Settings > Apps > Default apps > Digital assistant app**, tap **Default digital assistant app** and choose **None**. Google's instructions tie "Hey Google" and the touch shortcuts, such as holding the power button, to the Google app being your default assistant app, so this one change covers all of them. If you still use Gemini and only want it to stop popping up, change the one shortcut that bothers you instead: the power button, "Hey Google" or the lock screen.

Deleting the Gemini app doesn't do this on its own. Google's help page says removing the app doesn't remove Gemini as your default digital assistant. And if you hoped to go back to Google Assistant, that same page still lists a "Switch from Gemini to Google Assistant" section in its contents, but when we opened it on November 5, 2026, the section itself was gone.

We don't have an Android phone in our test setup, so every menu name below comes from Google's Gemini, Pixel and Google Play help pages and Samsung's US support page, all opened on November 5, 2026. We rendered Google's main page on the topic in Chromium to check which sections it actually contains. Menu names vary by phone maker, and Google says so on each page.

## How to turn off Gemini as your assistant completely

Setting your default digital assistant app to **None** stops Gemini from answering the assistant shortcuts on an Android phone. Google's [Gemini setup page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14554984?hl=en) ties those shortcuts to this setting: "If the Google app is your default Android assist app," Gemini opens when you say "Hey Google," press and hold the power button, touch and hold Home or swipe up from a bottom corner of the screen.

1. Open your phone's **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Apps**, then **Default apps**, then **Digital assistant app**.
3. Tap **Default digital assistant app**.
4. Choose **None**, or another assistant app if you have one.

If you can't find the menu, Google suggests typing "digital assistant app" into the search box at the top of Settings.

The list may not say Gemini at all. Google's [page on managing the Gemini app](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16938321?hl=en) explains that "Google" is selected "if you use either Google Assistant or Gemini as your default digital assistant app." So seeing **Google** there doesn't tell you which of the two answers, though with Google moving most phones to Gemini, it's most likely Gemini.

With **None** selected, the Gemini app still opens when you tap its icon, and Gemini inside Messages, Chrome and Gmail keeps its own switches, covered [further down](#gemini-in-google-messages-chrome-and-gmail). To bring the assistant back later, repeat the steps and pick **Google**.

## How to stop the power button from opening Gemini

On a Pixel, set **Settings > System > Gestures > Press and hold the power button** to **Power menu**. On a Samsung Galaxy phone, set **Settings > Advanced features > Side button > Long press** to **Power off menu**. Both changes leave Gemini on the phone and only change what a long press does.

**On a Pixel:**

1. Open **Settings** and tap **System**, then **Gestures**.
2. Tap **Press and hold the power button**. Google's Gemini page calls it **Press & hold power button**.
3. Choose **Power menu** instead of **Digital assistant**.

Google's [Pixel help page](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/7374159?hl=en) adds that on a Pixel 6 or later, holding the power and volume up buttons together for a few seconds brings up **Power off** on the screen.

**On a Samsung Galaxy:**

1. Open **Settings** and tap **Advanced features**.
2. Tap **Side button**.
3. Under **Long press**, choose **Power off menu**. Samsung's [side button guide](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS00084203/) lists three choices there: **Digital assistant**, **Bixby** and **Power off menu**.

Picking **Bixby** swaps one assistant for Samsung's own rather than turning the assistant off. Samsung's guide also notes that pressing the side and volume down buttons together opens the power off menu.

On other Android phones, Google's advice is to search Settings for "power," "side button" or "gesture" and look for the press-and-hold action.

## How to turn off "Hey Google" for Gemini

Turning off **Hey Google** in the Gemini app stops Gemini from opening when you say the phrase, while the app and the button shortcut keep working.

1. Open the **Gemini** app.
2. Tap **Menu** at the left, then your profile picture or initial.
3. Tap **Settings**, then **Talk to Gemini hands-free**.
4. Turn off **Hey Google**.

## How to stop Gemini from opening on the lock screen

The Gemini app's **Settings > Gemini on lock screen** page holds two switches: **Use Gemini without unlocking** and **Make calls and send messages without unlocking**. Turn off the first to keep Gemini from answering until you unlock the phone, and the second if you only want to stop calls and texts from the lock screen.

Google's [lock screen page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14576209?hl=en) lists what Gemini can do while your phone is locked: answer general questions, read and reply to messages in notifications, set alarms and timers, control smart home devices and, if you opted in, make calls and send texts. Requests involving personal content, such as reading your calendar, already ask you to unlock first.

## Can you switch back to Google Assistant?

On most Android phones, probably not. Google said in March 2025 that the classic Google Assistant would stop being available on most mobile devices, and its current Gemini help no longer explains how to switch back.

Google's [March 14, 2025 announcement](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/google-assistant-gemini-mobile/) said it was "upgrading more users on mobile devices from Google Assistant to Gemini; and later this year, the classic Google Assistant will no longer be accessible on most mobile devices or available for new downloads on mobile app stores." A footnote made one exception: phones that don't meet Gemini's minimum system requirements keep Google Assistant as it is.

The help page still points to a way back. Its contents list, at the top of Google's [page on managing the Gemini app](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16938321?hl=en), includes "Switch from Gemini to Google Assistant." When we loaded the page in Chromium on November 5, 2026, that link led nowhere: the page has sections for the other seven links in the list, but none for this one. What's left are the options in this guide.

Google's [Gemini setup page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14554984?hl=en) still describes a screen called **Digital assistants from Google**, reached from the Google app at **Settings > Google Assistant**, or from your phone's **Settings > Apps > Assistant**. Google presents it as the place to switch to Gemini, and the same section talks about "switching back to Gemini," which suggests the choice once ran both ways there. If your phone still has that screen and lists Google Assistant on it, that's where to look. We couldn't check which phones still do.

The switch reaches past the phone. According to the same page, Gemini also replaces Google Assistant on watches, headphones and earbuds paired with the phone, while smart displays, smart speakers, TVs, cars and locked Pixel Tablets keep answering "Hey Google" with Google Assistant.

## Does deleting the Gemini app turn Gemini off?

No. Google's help says "deleting the Gemini mobile app doesn't automatically remove Gemini as your device's default digital assistant," so set the assistant to **None** first if you want it gone from the shortcuts too.

To uninstall it, Google's [Android help on deleting apps](https://support.google.com/android/answer/13627402?hl=en) says to open **Google Play**, tap your profile icon, then **Manage apps & devices > Manage**, select the app and tap **Uninstall**. Some phones come with Gemini already installed, and Google's [Play help on pre-installed apps](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/13627571?hl=en) says some system apps can't be deleted, though "on some phones, you can turn them off" so they no longer appear in your app list. How to do that depends on your phone's maker. You can reinstall Gemini from Google Play at any time.

## Gemini in Google Messages, Chrome and Gmail

Gemini inside other Google apps has its own switches, and changing your phone's assistant doesn't touch them.

- **Google Messages:** tap your profile picture or initial at the top, then **Messages settings > Gemini in Messages**, and turn off **Show Gemini button**. If you also want to remove your Gemini chat, Google's [Gemini in Messages page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14599070?hl=en) says deleting it in Messages removes it from your Gemini activity as well, but deleting it from Gemini activity alone leaves the copy in Messages.
- **Chrome on Android:** Google's [Gemini in Chrome page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16283624?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) describes an **Ask Gemini** button that you opt in to the first time you use it, for signed-in users 18 and over on phones with Android 12 or later and at least 4GB of memory. Its Android version doesn't describe an off switch. On a computer, our guide to [turning off AI in Chrome](/posts/turn-off-ai-in-chrome) covers Gemini and the browser's other AI settings.
- **Gmail and Docs:** one setting, **Smart features in Google Workspace**, covers Gemini in Gmail and the other Workspace apps. Our guide to [turning off Gemini in Gmail](/posts/turn-off-gemini-in-gmail) matches each Gmail AI feature to its switch.
- **Android Auto:** our look at [Gemini's Personal Intelligence on Android Auto](/posts/gemini-android-auto-personal-intelligence) covers which Gemini settings carry over to the car.

## Stop Gemini from reading your screen or saving your chats

Two Gemini app settings control what Gemini sees and keeps, separate from how it opens: **Screen context** and **Keep Activity**.

- **Screen context:** when you open Gemini over another app, its **Ask about screen** button lets it use what's on the screen. Google's setup page says this needs **use text from screen** and **use screenshot** turned on under **Settings > Screen context** in the Gemini app, so turning them off stops it.
- **Keep Activity:** this setting, reached through **Gemini Apps Activity** in the menu, decides whether Google saves your Gemini chats to your account. Our guide to [turning off Gemini Apps Activity](/posts/turn-off-gemini-apps-activity) lists what stops working and what Google keeps anyway.
- **Location:** Gemini uses the Google app's location permission. Google's setup page points to **Settings > Location > App location permissions > Google**, where you can turn off **Use precise location** or pick a different access level.

## Which option should you pick?

![Six cards matching goals to Gemini settings on Android: change the press-and-hold action if Gemini pops up from the button, set the default digital assistant app to None for no assistant at all, turn off Hey Google to stop voice activation, turn off Keep Activity to stop saving chats, pick None before deleting the app, and Google Assistant only if the phone still offers it](./images/turn-off-gemini-on-android-phone/gemini-android-which-setting.webp)

- **Gemini keeps popping up by accident:** change the long press on the power or side button, and Gemini stays one tap away in its app.
- **You don't want any assistant:** set the default digital assistant app to **None**. It's the only setting that covers every shortcut at once, and it's easy to undo.
- **You use Gemini, just not out loud:** turn off **Hey Google** and leave everything else.
- **Your worry is privacy, not the shortcuts:** turn off **Keep Activity** and **Screen context**, and check the lock screen switches.
- **You want the app gone:** choose **None** first, then uninstall or disable it.

## FAQ

### How do I turn off Ask Gemini?

"Ask Gemini" is the name of Gemini's button in Chrome on Android. Google's Android instructions say you opt in the first time you use it, and they don't describe a way to turn it off afterward. When Gemini opens over another app from a shortcut instead, the shortcut settings above, or **None** as your assistant, stop it.

### Does turning off Gemini delete my past chats?

No. None of the shortcut settings delete anything. Past chats live in **Gemini Apps Activity**, where you delete them separately, and chats in Google Messages are deleted in Messages.
