Set up Exchange ActiveSync on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro Exchange ActiveSync lets you sync your Exchange account Mail, Contacts, Calendars, Reminders, and Notes with your Apple device. If you're not in a familiar location, you must first turn off Stolen Device Protection before you configure an Exchange account, and then re-enable it after configuration is complete. Learn more about Stolen Device Protection Add and configure your Exchange Account You can add your Exchange account in the Mail app on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro. Enter your Exchange email address Go to Settings, then tap Apps. Tap Mail. On iPhone or iPad, tap Mail Accounts. On Apple Vision Pro, tap Accounts. Tap Add Account, then tap Microsoft Exchange.
