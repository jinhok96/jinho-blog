End of support for legacy Outlook for Mac Applies To Outlook for Microsoft 365 for Mac Outlook 2024 for Mac Outlook 2021 for Mac Outlook 2019 for Mac As technology evolves, so must our tools to maintain efficiency, security, and user satisfaction. Starting October 2026, the legacy Outlook for Mac client will stop working against Exchange Online mailboxes. This transition marks a significant step towards a more robust, feature-rich, and secure email experience for all users. The legacy Outlook for Mac client will continue to work against Exchange On-Premises mailboxes until October 9, 2029 1 . The latest version of Outlook for Mac has been part of the Microsoft 365 suite for the past four years. Over 95% of Microsoft 365 users have already moved from legacy Outlook for Mac to this new version. Learn more about the new features and benefits in our Outlook for Mac Features , Outlook Blog, and Release Notes articles. For information on what's next, please follow the Microsoft 365 Roadmap for Outlook for Mac . Update as of October 6th, 2025 We recognize that some customers may be blocked from accessing certain features. If you are blocked, please contact support for assistance. In the meantime, to help mitigate this, Outlook for Mac will continue to respect admin preferences defined here . Switch to Outlook for Mac Legacy Outlook for Mac uses Exchange Web Services (EWS) in Exchange Online to deliver your email. The EWS service will be retired on October 1, 2026 . To avoid disruption to your email delivery and to help keep your data secure, we strongly recommend you switch from legacy Outlook for Mac to Outlook for Mac which uses modern technology designed for better performance, security, and reliability. Switch from legacy Outlook Open legacy Outlook for Mac, and select the toggle to turn off the Legacy Outlook switch. Note If your version of Outlook doesn't include a toggle, you may need to download the newer version from within the app at Help > Check for Updates , or from the Apple App Store . Get support with Outlook for Mac How you get support with Outlook for Mac depends on if you're using Outlook with a Microsoft account, or with a work or school account through your organization. If you're using a Microsoft account , to get support within the app, go to Help > Contact Support . If you're using a work or school , contact your organization's help desk who can initiate a support ticket with Unified Support (formerly Premier) on your behalf.
