19   through a $250,000,000.00 non-reversionary fund representing approximately 11% of estimated damages

20   for a class of approximately 36 million consumers. “[I]t is well-settled law that a proposed settlement may

21   be acceptable even though it amounts to only a fraction of the potential recovery that might be available

22   to the class members at trial.” In re MacBook Keyboard Litig., No. 5:18-CV-02813-EJD, 2023 WL

23   3688452, at *9 (N.D. Cal. May 25, 2023) (approving settlement of approximately 9% to 28% of the total

24   estimated damages). The Settlement here falls well within the acceptable range of Court-approved

25   settlements in consumer class actions, as depicted below.
