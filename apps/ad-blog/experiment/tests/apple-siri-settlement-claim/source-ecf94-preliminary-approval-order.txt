18          Before the Court is Plaintiffs’ Motion for Preliminary Approval of Class Action Settlement
19   (“Motion”). ECF No. 77. The Court held a hearing on the Motion on July 8, 2026. Having reviewed
20   the Motion, declarations submitted in support thereof, the proposed Settlement Agreement and
21   accompanying exhibits attached as Exhibit 1 (the “Settlement”), and applicable authority, the Court
22   finds that the Settlement is fair, adequate, and reasonable. Accordingly, with good cause appearing,
23   the Court hereby GRANTS preliminary approval of the Settlement and ORDERS as follows:
24          1.      Settlement Terms. The Court, for purposes of this Preliminary Approval Order,
25   adopts all defined terms as set forth in the Settlement.
26          2.      Jurisdiction. The Court has jurisdiction over the subject matter of the Action and
27   over all parties to the Action, including all members of the Settlement Class.

 1          3.      Preliminary Approval of Proposed Settlement. Subject to further consideration by
 2   the Court at the time of the Final Approval Hearing, the Court preliminarily approves the Settlement
 3   as fair, reasonable, and adequate to the Settlement Class, as falling within the range of possible final
 4   approval, and as meriting submission to the Settlement Class for its consideration. The Court also
 5   finds the Settlement: (a) is the result of serious, informed, non-collusive, arms-length negotiations,
 6   involving experienced counsel familiar with the legal and factual issues of this case; (b) appears to
 7   meet all applicable requirements of law, including Fed. R. Civ. P. 23; and (c) appears to meet the
 8   Northern District of California’s Procedural Guidance for Class Action Settlements. Therefore, the
 9   Court grants preliminary approval of the Settlement.
10          On May 19, 2026, named Plaintiffs Michael Accardi, Brian Beach, Sam Leone, Amanda
11   Marrocco, Adam Ragsdale (“Objecting Plaintiffs”), and their counsel, Executive Committee member
12   Pomerantz LLP filed an opposition to Plaintiffs’ Motion for Preliminary Approval. ECF No. 83.
13   Objecting Plaintiffs’ main criticism is that the Settlement “does not appropriately distinguish between
14   the vastly differing values of the claims of different class members” and accordingly, the Court should
15   consider creating subclass divisions. Id. at 1,3. The Court disagrees; the underlying value of the
16   Enhanced Siri features is the same across all Eligible Devices. Moreover, Objecting Plaintiffs failed
17   to raise this point or propose subclass in their own pleadings. See Accardi et al v. Apple Inc., 25-cv-
18   04160, ECF No. 1.
19          4.      Class Certification for Settlement Purposes Only. For purposes of the Settlement
20   only, the Court conditionally certifies the Settlement Class, as described below:
21   The Nationwide Class is defined as:
22
                    Purchasers of the Eligible Devices (iPhone 16, iPhone 16e, iPhone 16 Plus, iPhone 16
23                  Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max, iPhone 15 Pro, or iPhone 15 Pro Max) who reside in the
                    United States and purchased an Eligible Device in the United States for purposes other
24                  than resale, during the Class Period (June 10, 2024, to March 29, 2025).

25          Excluded from the Class are Apple Inc. (“Apple”); any entity in which Apple has a controlling
26   interest; Apple’s directors, officers, and employees; Apple’s legal representatives, successors, and
27   assigns. Also excluded from the Settlement Class are all judicial officers assigned to this case as well
28   as their staff and immediate family members.

 7          13.     Objection and Exclusion Deadline. Settlement Class Members who wish to object to
 8   the Settlement or to exclude themselves from the Settlement must do so by the Objection and
 9   Exclusion Deadline, which is 90 days from the Notice Date. Any Settlement Class Member who
10   submits an Exclusion Request and a Claim Form shall not be entitled to receive any monetary payment,
11   and his or her claim shall not be considered a Valid Claim and shall not be entitled to object to the
12   Settlement or appear at the Final Approval Hearing. All Settlement Class Members who do not submit
13   a timely, valid notice Exclusion Request will be bound by the Settlement and the Judgment, including
14   the release of any claims pursuant to the Settlement.
15          14.     Exclusion from the Settlement Class. To request to be excluded from the Settlement,
16   a member of the Settlement Class must timely, personally submit an individual written request for
17   exclusion by (a) mailing it with a postmark by the Exclusion Deadline to the designated Post Office
18   box established by the Claims Administrator or (b) emailing it to the Claims Administrator using the
19   email address provided on the Settlement Website no later than the Exclusion Deadline. To be effective,
20   the written Exclusion Request must include the following: (a) the requestor’s name, address and email
21   address; (b) the requestor’s individual signature; (c) the name and number of this Litigation (e.g.,
22   “Landsheft, et al. v. Apple Inc., Case No. 5:25-cv-02668 (N.D. Cal.)”); and (4) a clear request that the
23   Settlement Class Member would like to “opt out” or be excluded, by use of those or other words clearly
24   indicating a desire not to participate in the Settlement. One person may not opt out another person,
25   and so-called “class” or “mass” opt-outs shall not be permitted or recognized. No later than seven (7)
26   calendar days after the Objection and Exclusion Deadline, the Settlement Administrator will provide
27   Class Counsel and Apple Counsel with the number of Settlement Class Members who have timely and
28   validly excluded themselves from the Settlement.

 1           22.     Final Approval Hearing. The Court shall conduct a Final Approval Hearing to
 2   determine final approval of the Agreement on September 29, 2027, at 9:00 a.m. At the Final
 3   Approval Hearing, the Court shall address whether the proposed Settlement should be finally approved
 4   as fair, reasonable and adequate, whether the Final Approval Order and Judgment should be entered,
 5   and whether Class Counsel’s application for attorneys’ fees, costs, expenses and service award should
 6   be approved. Consideration of any application for an award of attorneys’ fees, costs, expenses and
 7   service award shall be separate from consideration of whether the proposed Settlement should be
 8   approved. The Court will not decide the amount of any service award or Class Counsel’s attorneys’
 9   fees until the Final Approval Hearing. The Final Approval Hearing may be adjourned or continued
10   without further notice to the Class.
