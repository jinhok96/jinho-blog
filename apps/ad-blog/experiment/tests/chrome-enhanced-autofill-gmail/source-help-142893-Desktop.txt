Fill out forms automatically in Chrome
To reduce manual data entry and improve accuracy, you can use Chrome to automatically fill out online forms with saved info, like your name, passwords, addresses, and payment info. It can also use your name and email address from your Google Account and certain info from your Google Wallet. This provides an easy and complete filling experience.
Chrome never shares your info without your permission. Learn how Chrome protects your autofill and password data.
If you would like to save your password info to Chrome, learn how to manage saved passwords.
If you're having issues with your saved info, learn how to fix issues with info saved to Chrome.
To use autofill, make sure you’re signed in to your Google Account in Chrome.
Tip: Not all autofill features are available in all regions.
Manage saved form info
Important: The save and fill info setting is on by default for Google Password Manager, payments, contact info, identity docs, and travel. If you've turned the save and fill setting off, autofill won't work for that info.
To access your saved info:
On your computer, open Chrome.
At the top right, select More Passwords and autofill.
You can also select Profile picture Passwords and autofill.
You can manage your autofill info for forms, like:
Google Password Manager
Payments
Contact info
Identity docs
Travel
Add, edit, or delete your saved autofill info in Chrome
If your payment method is saved in Google Wallet, you'll need to edit or delete it in Google Wallet.
Select the info you want to update, like payments, addresses, or contact info.
Under each category, you can turn Save and fill [info] on or off.
To add, edit, or delete info:
Add: At the top right of the info type, select Add.
Some menus have additional options and info types to choose from.
Edit or Delete: To the right of the saved info, select More Edit or Delete.
If you add, edit, or delete info and you’re signed in to Chrome with your Google Account, your changes will also show up on your other devices where you’re signed in to Chrome with the same account.
Tips:
When you submit forms with new or updated info, Chrome may prompt you to save this data to your Google Wallet.
When you’re signed into your Google Account, address info is saved in your Google Account. All other info, like identification and vehicle information, is saved in your Google Wallet.
If Use passes across Google is turned off in your Google Wallet settings, your info is saved locally to your device.
Under "Related Google services," you can also manage your Google Password Manager, Google Wallet, and Google Account.

Fix issues with autofill not working
If Chrome is not suggesting your saved info:
Check Chrome settings:
Make sure "Save and fill" is turned on for the info you want to autofill.
Review your Account:
Confirm the name and email shared from your Google Account for autofill at myaccount.google.com/personal-info.
Confirm the info you want to populate is saved, select More Passwords and autofill.
Enter your credentials again: If your login credentials fail, like passkeys or PINs, Chrome can’t access your sensitive info. Learn how to fix issues with passwords and passkeys.
Check website security:
If the website is secure, Chrome may not be able to detect certain fields in the form.
If the website is not secure enough, it may not be able to receive info from Chrome.
Check Google Wallet settings: To allow autofill to use info from Google Wallet, make sure Use passes across Google is turned on.
Delete your saved autofill form info in Chrome
To delete all of your addresses, payment methods, or other saved info in Chrome at the same time:
On your computer, open Chrome.
At the top right, select More .
Select Delete browsing data.
Choose a time range, like "Last hour" or "All time."
Under "Advanced," choose Autofill form data.
This method doesn't delete payment info and addresses stored in Google Pay, or info stored in Google Wallet. Learn how to remove info from Google Wallet.
Use enhanced autofill
Important: Enhanced autofill is currently available on Desktop, Android, and iOS for Gemini in Chrome users in the US who are 18 or older and have opted into Gemini Personal Intelligence.
Enhanced autofill uses data from your Gmail account, such as flights, vehicles, and orders, to fill out forms for you. Suggestions are displayed with the Gemini spark icon and will appear in a dropdown.
You can manage or turn off enhanced autofill by adjusting your Chrome settings:
On your computer, open Chrome.
At the top right, select More Settings Autofill and passwords.
Under "Autofill settings," select Enhanced autofill.
Turn "Find and fill with Gemini" on or off.
To see where Chrome found your information:
In the form field, select the suggestion dropdown.
Next to the Gemini suggestion , hover over the listed suggestion to view the menu.
Select a link to the original source. The email in Gmail will open in a new tab.
If Chrome suggests info you don't want to see or that is outdated:
In the form field, select the suggestion dropdown.
Next to the Gemini suggestion , hover over the listed suggestion to view the menu.
Select Remove from Chrome.
Tips:
Info pulled from Gmail can’t be edited in Chrome.
Removing suggestions from Chrome does not delete the original email. However, deleting the original email does remove the suggestion.
Fill more fields with smarter form understanding
Important: Not all websites and forms are compatible with smarter form understanding.
With smarter form understanding, Chrome is able to autofill forms faster for you.
To use this feature, make sure you're signed in to your Google Account in Chrome.
Turn Smarter form understanding on or off
Important: This setting is on unless you choose to turn it off.
On your computer, open Chrome.
At the top right, select More Settings Autofill and passwords.
Under "Autofill settings," select Autofill settings.
Turn Smarter form understanding on or off.
Turn re-authentication on or off
Re-authentication is on by default to provide extra protection for your sensitive info. Sensitive personally identifiable info is partially hidden. You must use a screen lock, fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN before Chrome has access to populate this info in forms.
If your device doesn't support biometrics, Chrome uses your default device screen lock like password or passcode instead.
On your computer, open Chrome.
At the top right, select More Settings Autofill and passwords.
Under “Autofill settings,” select Autofill settings.
Turn Verify it’s you when filling sensitive info on or off.
If this toggle is off, authentications like screen lock or biometric verification won’t appear when you attempt to autofill sensitive info.
