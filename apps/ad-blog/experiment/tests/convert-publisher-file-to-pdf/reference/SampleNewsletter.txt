This week in class, we have been discussing what it means to be an American. We had to divide into groups of 5 and brainstorm words and ideas that we associate with being American. 

Our ideas were recorded in a Microsoft® Visio® mind map. 

Next, we got to record ourselves singing the National Anthem on the computer. 

Then, we had to look up pictures on the Internet to match the ideas we wrote in our mind maps. That was great fun as we found lots of interesting pictures that we hadn't thought to include initially. 

We put everything together in a Microsoft PowerPoint® presentation. 

While our recording of the National Anthem played, our pictures that we downloaded with the words we had written down earlier flashed across the screen.
It was interesting watching what other groups had come up with in their presentation. We all had a great time learning.

Our Excursion to the Zoo
On Monday, we visited the zoo to learn about animal habitats. 
We caught the bus from school and arrived in time for recess.
Josh, the zoo keeper, told us all about the different habitats that animals live in.
My favorite was the reptile house. I learned that snakes can live on land, up trees and even in the water. 
We had a great time at the zoo learning all about what animals need in their environment.
