FAQs
Has Microsoft retired Publisher?

Yes. As of 1 October 2026, Publisher is no longer included with Microsoft 365. Perpetual licences, such as Office 2021 and Office LTSC 2021, continued to run until 13 October 2026, after which they stopped receiving security updates. Office 2024 does not include Publisher.

How do we open a .pub file without Publisher?

Affinity imports .pub files directly, keeping your text, images and layout as editable objects.

Can I still open my Publisher files and edit the contents?

Yes, in Affinity. Opening a .pub file imports its content into a new Affinity document that you can keep editing, saving and exporting. It doesn't save back to the .pub format. From that point, you're working in an Affinity file, not a Publisher one.

What's the best way to convert a Publisher file to PDF?

Open the .pub file in Affinity and export to PDF, or to PDF/X if it's going to a commercial printer. Your layout will be editable, and you can make changes before you export.

Will my file look exactly the same?

Close, but maybe not identical. The structure comes across — text, images, shapes, page layout — and it's all editable, so anything that shifts is quick to put right. A few things need redoing by hand: tables come in as text rather than as a table, decorative borders and gradient fills don't carry over, and some older clip art may not appear (e.g. EMF). Everything else is there and ready to work on.

Does Affinity handle data merge, similar to Publisher's mail merge?

Yes. Affinity's Data Merge flows a spreadsheet or database directly into a layout, populating names, numbers and images across every page, for certificates, labels and personalised documents, the same use case Publisher's mail merge served.
