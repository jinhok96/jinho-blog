About this extension
At DuckDuckGo, we believe AI should be optional. That's why we've created this extension, which sets your default search engine to DuckDuckGo, but to a version that doesn't use AI tools.

Searching with the DuckDuckGo No-AI Search works the same as searching with DuckDuckGo Search, except AI-generated images are filtered out of image results by default, and all other AI features have been removed completely, all while delivering the trusted DuckDuckGo results you love.

Feature highlights:
- All the great features of DuckDuckGo Search, minus AI tools.
- Ideal for straightforward, seamless searching without AI tools.
- Perfect if you never want AI to help you search.

Considering AI?
If you prefer more flexibility, check out our full-featured extension, DuckDuckGo Search & Tracker Protection, where you can easily toggle AI settings on and off according to your preferences. It also blocks trackers as you browse for even more privacy. Get started at https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/duckduckgo-for-firefox/
