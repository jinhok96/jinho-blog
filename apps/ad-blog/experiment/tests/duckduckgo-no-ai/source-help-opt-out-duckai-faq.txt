I don't want anything to do with AI. How can I opt out of this?

Duck.ai is a completely optional feature and you can control where it appears in the DuckDuckGo browser, or turn it off entirely. In the DuckDuckGo browser menu, go to Settings > AI Features to customize where Duck.ai shows up.
