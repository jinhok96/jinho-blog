Opting Out of DuckDuckGo AI Features

At DuckDuckGo, we recognize not everyone wants AI in their browser or search results. That’s why all our AI features are optional and can be turned off or tuned down.

Turn off Duck.ai in the DuckDuckGo browser

Go to Settings > AI Features
Turn off Duck.ai to completely disable Duck.ai features in the browser

Turn off Duck.ai in DuckDuckGo search

Go to Search Settings > AI Features
Turn off Duck.ai
Select Save and Exit to refresh DuckDuckGo search with Duck.ai disabled

Turn off Search Assist

Go to Search Settings > AI Features
Select Never from the Search Assist dropdown options
Select Save and Exit to refresh DuckDuckGo search with Search Assist disabled
