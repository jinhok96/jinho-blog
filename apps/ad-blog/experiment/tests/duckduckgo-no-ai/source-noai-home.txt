DuckDuckGo NoAI: Private Search Engine Without AI

Search privately by default, without AI features. Searching with DuckDuckGo No-AI Search works the same as DuckDuckGo Search, except AI-generated images are filtered out of image results by default, and all other AI features have been removed completely, all while delivering the trusted DuckDuckGo results you love.

With DuckDuckGo, AI is optional and private.
