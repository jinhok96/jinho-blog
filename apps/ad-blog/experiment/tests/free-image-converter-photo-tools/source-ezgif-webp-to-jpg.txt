Online WebP to JPG (JPEG) Converter: Free, No Watermark
WebP is a modern image format developed by Google for efficient compression and high-quality visuals on the web.
While WebP offers smaller file sizes compared to JPEG, not all browsers, image viewers, email clients, and
websites fully support it. Converting WebP to JPG ensures your images work everywhere: from social media
uploads to email attachments and print workflows.
This free online converter handles both static and animated WebP files. For animated WebP images, it generates
a sequence of JPG frames that you can save individually or download as a ZIP archive. Since WebP supports alpha
transparency but JPG does not, you can choose a background color to replace transparent areas. The quality slider
lets you find the perfect balance between file size and visual fidelity.
How to Convert WebP to JPG:
Upload Your WebP File: Drag and drop your WebP images into the bulk converter area to process locally in your browser, or switch to server-side mode for single-file server conversion.
Adjust Settings: Set the JPG quality factor (0-100) and choose a background color for transparent images.
Convert: Download your converted image, or continue editing it with our other tools.
When to Convert WebP to JPG
Email attachments: Most email clients display JPG images natively but may not support WebP.
Social media: Some platforms still require JPG or PNG uploads.
Print and publishing: Print workflows typically work with JPG/TIFF, not WebP.
Older software: Image editors and viewers that haven't been updated may not open WebP files.
Website compatibility: Serving JPG as a fallback for browsers that don't support WebP.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the WebP image format?
WebP is an image format developed by Google that provides both lossy and lossless compression. WebP files are
typically 25-35% smaller than equivalent JPEG images at the same visual quality. WebP also supports transparency
(alpha channel) and animation, making it a versatile format for web use.
Why convert WebP to JPG?
While WebP is efficient for web delivery, JPG (JPEG) remains the most universally supported image format.
Converting WebP to JPG ensures compatibility with all devices, applications, and platforms: including older
browsers, image editors, and print services that may not recognize WebP files.
Can I convert multiple WebP files at once?
Yes! This tool lets you batch convert multiple WebP files simultaneously right in your browser. All converted JPGs can be downloaded as a single ZIP file.
Is this converter completely free?
Yes, this tool is 100% free to use with no watermarks, no signup required, and no conversion limits.
Will the conversion reduce image quality?
Since both WebP (lossy mode) and JPG are lossy formats, there is a minor quality loss during conversion.
You can minimize this by setting the JPG quality slider to a higher value (90-100). For lossless output,
consider converting to PNG format instead.
Can my browser convert WebP to JPG without a tool?
Modern browsers can display WebP images, and you can sometimes "Save As" JPG through the right-click menu.
However, this doesn't work reliably for all images, doesn't let you control quality, doesn't handle
animated WebP, and can't process multiple files. This converter handles all those cases consistently.
