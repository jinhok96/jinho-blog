Talk to Gemini or Google Assistant
Gemini is replacing Google Assistant on most mobile devices. Learn more about
what you can do with Gemini
privacy
protection of minors
and
availability
for supported devices, languages and territories. Gemini will understand the same commands as Google Assistant and also give you the option to speak naturally. We will share more details soon as we continue our work to deliver a seamless transition to upgrade Assistant users to Gemini in 2026.
You can talk to Gemini or Google Assistant to get more done hands-free and control Android Auto in
compatible cars
When you talk to Gemini, you can speak naturally without the use of traditional voice commands or phrases. Gemini can follow your conversation and understand questions that are detailed and complex.
Follow these steps to talk to Gemini or Google Assistant:
Choose one of three ways to start:
Say "Hey Google."
Tap the microphone
Press and hold the voice command button on your steering wheel.
Wait until you hear the tone.
Say what you'd like to do.
Find examples of things you can do with Gemini or Google Assistant:
Make a call
Send a message
Get driving directions
Listen to music
You can also say “Hey Google, let’s talk” or “Hey Google, let’s talk live” to brainstorm, explore, and learn something new with Gemini Live. For example, you can say things such as:
“I'm driving to St. Louis for a wedding. Can you be my tour guide and share some fun facts about it?”
“The rehearsal dinner is at the groom’s parents’ house, and I'd like to get them a small gift. What are some good ideas?”
“I will give a speech at the wedding. Let's practice.”
