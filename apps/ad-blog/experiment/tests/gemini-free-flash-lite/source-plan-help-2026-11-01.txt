Manage your Google AI plan from Gemini Apps

You can upgrade to a Google AI plan for higher limits and priority access to some features in Gemini Apps. You can also cancel or change your plan at any time. Learn more about Gemini Apps limits & feature availability.

Important:

This article is for users managing Gemini Apps for a personal Google Account. If you’re using a work or school Google Account, learn more about using Gemini Apps with a work or school account.
To use Gemini Apps with a Google AI plan in most countries, you must be 13 (or the applicable age in your country) or over.
To use Gemini Apps with a Google AI plan in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom, you must be 18 years of age or older.
Some features may not be available for users under the age of 18 or 13 (or the applicable age in your country). In the help center, you will find additional information about age requirements.

Upgrade to a Google AI plan in Gemini Apps

Important: Some Google AI plans are only available in certain countries. Learn more about plans and availability.

You can subscribe to a Google AI plan through Google One.

Upgrade to a Google AI plan (without a current plan)
Go to gemini.google.com.
At the bottom, click Settings & help   View Subscriptions.
Select the Google AI plan you want.
Follow the on-screen instructions.

Tip: When you subscribe to the plan, you’ll need to select or provide a form of payment. Learn how to manage your Google payment info.

Upgrade your Google AI plan (from a current plan)
Go to gemini.google.com.
At the bottom, click Settings & help .
Click the available upgrade option.
Follow the on-screen instructions.

Choose a different Gemini model to use

Important: To switch between models in Gemini Apps, you must be signed in. Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps.

Gemini Apps use auto model selection to select the most appropriate available model for your prompt.

If you want to manually select a different available model, you can select it from the menu.  Learn how to manually switch between Gemini models. 

Change your plan

To switch to a different Google One plan:

On your computer, go to Google One.
Click Manage membership  Change membership plan.
Choose your new plan.
To upgrade: Select a plan under "Upgrade options."
To downgrade: Select a plan under "Downgrade options."
If there’s no downgrade option, you're on the lowest plan.
To review the membership details, click See all benefits.
To confirm the new plan price and details, click Yes, continue.
Select your payment method.
Click Subscribe.

Cancel your plan

You can cancel your plan in Google One settings.

Go to gemini.google.com.
At the bottom, click Settings & help   Manage subscription. This opens Google One settings, where you can cancel your plan.
Click Cancel membership  Cancel.
