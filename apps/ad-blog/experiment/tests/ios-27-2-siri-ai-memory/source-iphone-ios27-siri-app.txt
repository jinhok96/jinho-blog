Siri AI: Use the Siri app on iPhone

When you turn on Siri AI (Beta), you have a dedicated Siri app that brings all your conversations together in one place, so you can ask a question on your iPhone and pick up where you left off on your other Apple devices when you’re signed in to the same Apple Account. You can also pin conversations for easy access or start a new one.

	

Siri AI is available in beta on iOS 27 or later on an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later with Siri and device language set to a supported language. Available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish to start. Siri AI will not initially be available in the EU on iOS. Apple Intelligence and Siri AI are not available in China mainland. Siri AI is not available for users under 13. Usage limits may apply.* 

Start or continue a conversation

Tap  to start a new conversation, or tap a conversation you want to continue.

Do any of the following:

Add photos: Tap , tap  Photos, then select a photo to add to the conversation.

Add files: Tap , tap  Files, then select a file to add to the conversation.

Ask follow-up questions: Enter follow-up questions.

View your conversation history

You can view your conversation history, which syncs across platforms when you’re signed in to the same Apple Account.

Go to the Siri app  on your iPhone.

If the Siri app opens to a new conversation, tap  to see your conversation history.

To change how your conversation history is displayed, tap  in the top-right corner, then tap  Grid or  List.

Search and pin conversations

Go to the Siri app  on your iPhone.

If the Siri app opens to a new conversation, tap  to see your conversation history.

Do any of the following:

Search a conversation: Tap , enter what you’re searching for, then tap  on the keyboard.

Pin conversations: Touch and hold the conversation you want to pin, then tap  Pin.

Rename conversations

Go to the Siri app  on your iPhone.

If the Siri app opens to a new conversation, tap  to see your conversation history.

Touch and hold a conversation, then tap  Rename.

Enter the new name for the conversation, then tap OK.

Delete Siri conversations

Go to the Siri app  on your iPhone.

Do one of the following:

Delete one Siri conversation: Touch and hold the conversation you want to delete, tap  Delete, then tap Delete to confirm.

Delete multiple Siri conversations: Tap  in the top-right corner, tap Select, then select the conversations you want to delete. Tap , then tap Delete to confirm.

Change Siri app settings

In Siri settings, you can choose what appears when you open the Siri app and how long to keep conversations.

Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.

Tap Siri.

Do any of the following:

Choose what appears when you open the Siri app: Tap Opens To, then choose Recent Conversations or A New Conversation.

Choose how long to keep conversations: Tap Keep Conversations, then choose an option.

Note: If you choose 30 days or 1 year, older conversations are permanently deleted.

Choose a conversation preview length: Tap Preview, then choose an option.

To keep Siri app conversations up to date across all your Apple devices, see Set up iCloud for Siri across all your devices.

You can choose which Siri features you can use and which content Siri can access in Screen Time settings. See Block features or content with Screen Time.
