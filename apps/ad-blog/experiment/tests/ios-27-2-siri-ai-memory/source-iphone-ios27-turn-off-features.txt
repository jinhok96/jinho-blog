Turn off and restrict access to Apple Intelligence features on iPhone

You can turn off Apple Intelligence features like Siri AI (Beta), summarization features, and more. For more information on parental controls, see Apple Devices for Kids and Families.

Note: Apple Intelligence on iOS 27 or later is available on an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later. Apple Intelligence is not available in all languages or regions. Usage limits may apply.*

To change these settings, go to the Settings app  on your iPhone, then do any of the following:

Option

	

Description




Siri AI (Beta)

	

Tap Siri, scroll down, then tap Turn Off Siri. Tap Turn Off Siri again to confirm.

Note: Siri AI is available in beta on iOS 27 or later on an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later with Siri and device language set to a supported language. Available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish to start. Siri AI will not initially be available in the EU on iOS. Apple Intelligence and Siri AI are not available in China mainland. Siri AI is not available for users under 13. Usage limits may apply.*


	

To use Siri Classic instead of Siri AI, follow the steps above to turn off Siri. Tap Turn on Siri, then tap Siri Classic.




Messages summaries

	

Tap Apps, tap Messages, then turn off Summarize Messages.




Email summaries

	

Tap Apps, tap Mail, then turn off Summarize Message Previews.




Notification summaries

	

Tap Notifications, tap Summarize Notifications, then turn off Summarize Notifications.




Priority notifications

	

Tap Notifications, tap Prioritize Notifications, then turn off Prioritize Notifications.




Personalized Smart Replies in Mail

	

Tap Apps, tap Mail, then turn Personalize Smart Replies on or off.




Voicemail suggestions in Phone

	

Tap Apps, tap Phone, then turn Suggestions in Voicemail on or off.




Call Context cards

	

See Exclude apps from Siri, Search, and suggestions.




Writing Prompts in Journal

	

Tap Apps, tap Journal, then turn Get Writing Prompts on or off.




Restrict access to Apple Intelligence features in Screen Time

	

See Block or allow Siri and Apple Intelligence features for restricting access for yourself. For your child, see Block or allow access to Siri and Apple Intelligence on your kid’s device in Apple Devices for Kids and Families.




Change how you see suggestions from your apps

	

See Turn Suggestions on or off.

*Apple Intelligence is available with Siri and device language set to Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, or Vietnamese. Some features may not be available in all regions or languages. Some devices may not be available in all regions. Siri AI is available in beta in iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27 and requires an Apple Intelligence–enabled device set to a supported language. Available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish to start. Siri AI will not be initially available in the EU on iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS. Siri AI is not available for users under 13. Certain Apple Intelligence features that rely on server-side models are subject to daily usage limits, including but not limited to Siri AI, Intelligent Photo Editing Tools, Image Playground, and AFM 3 Cloud models in Shortcuts. Daily limits may vary by feature, request complexity, system demand, system policies, and other factors. Expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future. Use of these features is subject to the Apple Intelligence Terms and Conditions. Learn more at support.apple.com/148587.
