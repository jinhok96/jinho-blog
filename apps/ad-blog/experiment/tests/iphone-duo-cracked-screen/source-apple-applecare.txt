Questions? Answers.
What is the difference between the limited warranty and AppleCare coverage?

Most Apple products come with one year of hardware coverage through the limited warranty. AppleCare plans extend that hardware coverage and provide additional features like accidental damage protection and 24/7 priority support.

What is the difference between AppleCare+ and AppleCare One?

AppleCare+ covers a single product. With AppleCare One, you can cover multiple Apple products in a single plan, including new devices and eligible devices you already own.

How do I buy AppleCare?

You can start an AppleCare+ or AppleCare One Individual plan when buying a new product with Apple in person, online, or through the Apple Store app. For 60 days after a new Apple purchase, you can get a new plan through the Settings app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. You can also upgrade your existing plan to AppleCare One Individual or Family on your device. When you switch plans, we’ll automatically cancel your current one and refund any amount owed.

Which Apple products are eligible to add to an AppleCare One plan?

Any product that was recently purchased or is currently covered by AppleCare+ can be covered in your AppleCare One plan. You can also add devices you already own that are in good condition (a diagnostic check and/or inspection may be required) and four years old or newer or any headphones one year old or newer.

Can I add my family’s devices to my AppleCare One plan?

Yes. AppleCare One Family covers all eligible devices for up to six people in your family. You can set up Family Sharing in the Settings app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac and invite family members to join.

How do I add or remove devices from my AppleCare One plan?

You can update your AppleCare One plan in Settings > General > AppleCare & Warranty. Tap AppleCare & Warranty to add, remove, or swap devices. Adjust your plan after you upgrade to the latest version of a device you already own or as needed, as long as it meets the eligibility requirements and is linked to your Apple Account. With AppleCare One Family, all of your family’s eligible devices are automatically added and your existing plans are upgraded for you. For more details, visit support.apple.com/en-us/122224.

Which products include AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss?

Theft and loss coverage is included for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch with both your AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss and AppleCare One plans.

Does AppleCare cover accessories?

When you protect your iPad with an AppleCare+ or AppleCare One plan, you’ll get coverage for accidental damage for one Apple Pencil and one Apple‑branded iPad keyboard. All plans also cover any in-box cables and power adapters that came with your covered Apple product.

How can I get my device repaired?

You can get your device repaired a few different ways. You can mail it in using a prepaid shipping label from Apple. Or you can bring it in to an Apple Store or any of the over 5,000 Apple Authorized Service Providers. Start a repair by visiting support.apple.com/repair, downloading the Apple Support app, or calling 1-800-692-7753.

How fast can I get my device repaired?

It depends on your product and the type of repair. Same-day service is available for iPhone in most major metropolitan areas. For most other products, we offer Express Replacement Service, where we’ll send you a device so you don’t have to wait for a repair. For all available service options, please visit support.apple.com/repair.

What do I do if my device is lost or stolen?

If your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch was lost or stolen, you can get started on filing a claim at mysupport.apple.com/theftandloss. Your replacement will be shipped to you and cannot be picked up at a store. If you are traveling when you lose your iPhone, we can ship your replacement to any of the countries where theft and loss is offered. For more details, visit support.apple.com/en-us/108053.
