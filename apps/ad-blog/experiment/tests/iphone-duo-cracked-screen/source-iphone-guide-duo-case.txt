Adjust your display when you put a case on iPhone Duo

If you use a case or frame that partially covers the left edge of the outer display on iPhone Duo, you can inset the display edge so content isn’t hidden beneath the edge of the case.

Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.

Tap Display & Brightness.

Turn on Shift Left Edge.
