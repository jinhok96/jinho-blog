Use Apple Pencil (USB-C) with iPhone Duo

iPhone Duo works with Apple Pencil (USB-C), sold separately. Other Apple Pencil models aren’t compatible with iPhone Duo.

Pair and charge Apple Pencil (USB-C)

Slide open the end of Apple Pencil, then connect it to the USB-C charge cable that came with your iPhone Duo.

Connect the other end of the USB-C charge cable to the USB-C connector on iPhone Duo.

Do any of the following:

Pair Apple Pencil: Tap “Tap to Connect.”

After you pair Apple Pencil, you can disconnect it from the USB-C charge cable. Apple Pencil stays paired until you restart your iPhone Duo, turn off Bluetooth®, or pair Apple Pencil with another device. To use it again, repeat these steps to pair it.

Charge Apple Pencil: Apple Pencil charges while it’s plugged into your iPhone Duo.

Tip: You can also charge Apple Pencil using the USB-C charge cable and a USB-C power adapter.

Check the battery level of Apple Pencil

Do any of the following:

Check the Batteries widget. See Add, edit, and remove widgets on iPhone.

Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone, then tap Apple Pencil.

Change Apple Pencil settings

Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.

Tap Apple Pencil, then do any of the following:

Draw only with Apple Pencil: Turn on Only Draw with Apple Pencil. You can still use your finger to scroll and select.

Turn handwriting into typed text: Turn on Scribble.

Practice with Scribble: Tap Try Scribble.
