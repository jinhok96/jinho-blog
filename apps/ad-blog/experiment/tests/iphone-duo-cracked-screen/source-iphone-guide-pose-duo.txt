Pose iPhone Duo

iPhone Duo has an outer display that’s comfortable to hold and use while on the go or for quick tasks. When iPhone Duo is open, you can use the expansive inner display to immerse yourself in your content or to be more productive. You can also partially fold iPhone Duo and set it down—useful for features like StandBy and taking photos at a distance.

Open or close iPhone Duo

Open  : Unfold iPhone Duo to use the larger inner display—for reading, watching, and using two apps at the same time. See Open two apps in Split View.

Close  : Fold iPhone Duo closed to use the outer display for quick, everyday tasks without unfolding it.

Partially fold or stand up iPhone Duo

Partially fold iPhone Duo like a book  so the inner display faces you in landscape. You can hold it and use it this way, or you can place it down to use StandBy or take photos with Smart Take.

Partially fold iPhone Duo and place it down like a tent  so the outer display faces you in landscape. Use this position to glance at information from a distance, or to face the display toward someone you’re photographing. See Use StandBy on iPhone to view information at a distance, Get children and pets to look at the iPhone Duo camera, or Show your subject a photo preview on iPhone Duo.

Partially fold iPhone Duo and set it down in a seated position  so the inner display faces you in portrait. Use this position to watch video from a distance with playback controls at the bottom, or to take a FaceTime call without holding iPhone Duo. See Use both displays on iPhone Duo to let someone join with Duo FaceTime.
