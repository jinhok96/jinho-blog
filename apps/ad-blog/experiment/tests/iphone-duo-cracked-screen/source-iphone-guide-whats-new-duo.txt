What’s new with iPhone Duo

iPhone Duo opens to reveal the largest iPhone display, and unlocks new ways to use iPhone. Learn more about how you can use it.

Multitask with ease

With iPhone Duo open, you can use Split View to work with two apps at the same time. You can also open multiple windows of the same app—such as two Safari tabs or your Mail inbox and a draft.

Open two apps in Split View on iPhone Duo

View information from a distance with StandBy

Partially open iPhone Duo and set it on a table to see the time, your widgets, and photos at a glance, without picking up your iPhone. You can choose which positions turn on StandBy, add a face, and customize other options.

Use StandBy on iPhone to view information at a distance

Make iPhone Duo your personal photographer

Set iPhone Duo on a table or flat surface, then start a session in Camera. Get into position and strike a pose or hold still—iPhone Duo detects when you’re posing and automatically takes photos for you, so you can be in the shot. No tapping required.

Take photos with Smart Take on iPhone Duo

New ways to shoot

When iPhone Duo is open, the outer display gives you new ways to use Camera. Point the rear cameras toward yourself to see the controls and a preview before you take a selfie. Duo Preview shows your subject how they appear in the frame, or Kid Cue plays a fun animation to get children and pets to look toward the camera.

Take a selfie on iPhone Duo

Show your subject a photo preview on iPhone Duo

Get children and pets to look at the iPhone Duo camera

Share both camera views on a FaceTime call

During a FaceTime call on iPhone Duo, Dual Capture shows the person you’re calling both your face and a second view, together in one layout. With Duo FaceTime, someone next to you can join the call on the outer display with their own view.

Use both displays on iPhone Duo to let someone join with Duo FaceTime

Write and draw with Apple Pencil (USB-C)

iPhone Duo lets you use Apple Pencil (USB-C) with iPhone for the first time. Use Apple Pencil to annotate, draw, mark up, and edit in the apps you use every day, or even use Scribble to write instead of type in any text field.
