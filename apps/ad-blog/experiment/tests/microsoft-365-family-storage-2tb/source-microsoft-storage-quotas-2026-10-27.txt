Note
Starting October 8, 2026, new Microsoft 365 Family and Premium subscriptions, including expired subscriptions that you repurchase, include 2 TB of shared OneDrive cloud storage. New Microsoft 365 Pro subscriptions include 5 TB of shared OneDrive cloud storage.
Starting May 2, 2027, existing Microsoft 365 Family and Premium subscriptions include 2 TB of shared cloud storage when the subscription renews. On November 1, 2027, existing Microsoft 365 Pro subscriptions automatically include 5 TB of shared cloud storage.

What happens when you exceed your Microsoft storage quota
If you exceed your Microsoft storage quota, some Microsoft services stop working.
OneDrive:  You can't upload, edit, or sync new files, including files from your camera roll. Your existing files remain, but they're read-only.
Outlook.com:  You can't send or receive email if you exceed either your cloud storage quota or mailbox storage quota. Incoming emails are returned to the sender and must be resent after you free up or buy more storage.
Microsoft Teams:  You can't send or receive messages that contain attachments.
After three months:  If you remain over your OneDrive storage limit, your OneDrive account is frozen.
After six months:  If you remain over your OneDrive storage limit, Microsoft might delete your OneDrive and all files in it. Deleted files can't be recovered.
Tip
Your OneDrive taskbar or menu bar icon shows the status of your storage:
Cloud icon with a yellow warning triangle
Your OneDrive storage is nearly full.
Cloud icon with a red circle and white minus sign
You exceeded your storage allowance.
Manage your storage
To prevent or resolve storage limit issues, you can buy more storage or free up space.
Buy more storage
If you use the free 5-GB Microsoft storage plan, buy a Microsoft 365 subscription to increase your storage quota.
Buy a Microsoft 365 subscription
If you have a Microsoft 365 Basic subscription, switch to a Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, or Pro subscription.
Switch your subscription
If you have a Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, or Pro subscription, Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, and Pro subscriptions can be extended up to 10 TB to give everyone  even more OneDrive storage . Only the subscription account holder can add storage. For shared subscriptions, all members share the additional storage.
Add OneDrive cloud storage
After you purchase additional OneDrive storage, your new quota can take up to 24 hours to appear. If your quota hasn't updated after 48 hours,  contact support .
