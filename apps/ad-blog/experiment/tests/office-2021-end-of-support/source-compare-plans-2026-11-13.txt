What’s the difference between Office 2024 (one-time purchase) and Microsoft 365 (subscription)?

Office 2024 is sold as a one-time purchase, which means you pay a single, up-front cost to get Office apps for one computer. One-time purchases are available for both PCs and Macs. However, there are no upgrade options, which means if you plan to upgrade to the next major release, you'll have to buy it at full price.

Microsoft 365 Personal, Microsoft 365 Family, Microsoft 365 Premium, and Microsoft 365 Pro are subscriptions that include powerful productivity apps and creativity tools with AI-powered features. In addition to desktop versions of popular Microsoft 365 apps like Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and Outlook with Microsoft Copilot,1 you also get spacious cloud storage, advanced security, and cloud-connected features that let you collaborate on files in real time. With a subscription, you'll always have the latest features, fixes, and security updates along with ongoing tech support at no extra cost. You can choose to pay for your subscription on a monthly or yearly basis, and use your apps on multiple PCs, Macs, tablets, and phones.

What's the difference between Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, and Premium?

Microsoft 365 Personal is for one person.

Microsoft 365 Family extends many of the same benefits to multiple people in a household.

Microsoft 365 Premium includes extensive AI usage and access to exclusive Copilot features.1

The right subscription depends on whether you're purchasing for yourself, your family, or need more advanced capabilities.

What's included in Microsoft 365 Premium that you won't get with Personal or Family?
If you primarily want access to desktop versions of productivity apps with Copilot,1 advanced security, and cloud storage, Microsoft 365 Personal or Microsoft 365 Family may be enough. If you want extensive AI usage and access to exclusive Copilot features,1 Microsoft 365 Premium can provide added value to help you tackle demanding tasks, from researching business ideas and analyzing complex data to creating high-impact presentations.
What is the best Microsoft 365 subscription for freelancers and self-employed professionals?
For most freelancers, solopreneurs, and side business owners looking to maximize productivity and protect their work, Microsoft 365 Premium is the recommended choice. It provides extensive AI usage and exclusive Copilot features,1 advanced security, 2 TB of cloud storage, and productivity apps with Copilot1 built in to help manage clients, projects, finances, and business communications. Those who need 1 TB of cloud storage, advanced security, and productivity apps with Copilot1 can choose Microsoft 365 Personal, while Microsoft 365 Family is a good fit for households that want to share subscription benefits1 across multiple users.
Which Microsoft 365 subscription is best for students?
For students who want the ultimate AI and productivity experience, Microsoft 365 Premium is an excellent choice. It combines extensive AI usage and exclusive Copilot features,1 advanced security, spacious cloud storage, and productivity apps with Copilot1 built in that can help students stay organized, manage coursework, collaborate on projects, and tackle academic responsibilities with confidence. Students looking for cloud storage, advanced security, and productivity apps with Copilot1 can also choose Microsoft 365 Personal.
Do I keep control of my documents with a Microsoft 365 subscription?
Yes. Documents that you have created belong fully to you. You can choose to store them online on OneDrive or locally on your PC or Mac.
If I’m not paying for a subscription, what do I get for free?
Visit learn more about free apps.
Can I share my AI benefits with others?
AI features included in Microsoft 365 Family, Microsoft 365 Premium, and Microsoft 365 Pro plans are only available to the subscription owner and cannot be shared with others.
Where can I learn more about Copilot?
Visit our Copilot help & learning site to start using Copilot today.
What are the usage limits?
Your Microsoft 365 Personal, Family, Premium, or Pro subscription unlocks higher AI usage limits to experience and engage with Copilot in your Microsoft 365 apps, the Microsoft Copilot app, and beyond. Learn more about credits.
