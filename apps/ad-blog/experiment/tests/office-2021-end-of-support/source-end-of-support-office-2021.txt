 End of support for Office 2021 Support for Office 2021 ended on October 13, 2026 and there will be no extension and no extended security updates. All of your Office 2021 apps may continue to function. However, you could expose yourself to serious and potentially harmful security risks. Buy or try Microsoft 365 Tip Not sure what version of Office you have? See Find details for other versions of Office to help you determine what version you're currently running. What end of support means for you after October 13, 2026 Microsoft will no longer provide technical support, bug fixes, or security fixes for Office 2021 vulnerabilities which may be subsequently reported or discovered. This includes security updates which can help protect your PC from harmful viruses, spyware, and other malicious software. You'll no longer receive Office 2021 software updates. You'll no longer receive phone or chat technical support. No further updates to support content will be provided and most online help content will be retired. What are my options? We recommend you upgrade Office. Your options to upgrade will depend if you're using Office 2021 at home or if your version of Office 2021 is managed by the IT department or the IT admin at your work or school . Office 2021 at home Office 2021 at work or school If you're using Office 2021 at home (such as, Office Home & Student 2021 or Office Home & Business 2021), you may still be able to use it, but we recommend you upgrade to a newer version of Office so you can stay up to date with all the latest features, patches, and security updates. To learn more about upgrading see How do I upgrade Office? You can also buy or try the latest version of Office now. Buy or try Microsoft 365 For individuals at work: If your version of Office 2021 is managed by your work or school, contact your IT Help Desk about how to upgrade. Your IT department will likely have their own upgrade plan. For IT Pros and Microsoft 365 admins: If you're an admin still running Office 2021 in your organization, we strongly recommend that you upgrade your users to the latest version of Office as soon as possible.
