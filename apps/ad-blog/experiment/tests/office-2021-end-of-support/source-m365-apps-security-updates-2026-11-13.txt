Release notes for Microsoft Office security updates
Summarize this article for me

These release notes provide information about security fixes that are included in updates to Microsoft Office.

This information applies to Microsoft 365 Apps for enterprise, Microsoft 365 Apps for business, Office 2019, Office LTSC 2021, Office 2021, Office LTSC 2024, and Office 2024.

 Important

Support for Office 2019 ended on October 14, 2025. However, at Microsoft’s sole discretion, we may choose to issue one or more updates to Office 2019 after October 14, 2025. If you haven't already begun to upgrade your environment to a newer version of Office, we recommend that you start now. For more information, see Plan an upgrade from older versions of Office to Microsoft 365 Apps.
