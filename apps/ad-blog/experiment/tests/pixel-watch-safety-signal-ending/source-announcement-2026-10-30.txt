End of Support for Safety Signal on Google Pixel Watch
Announcement
Hi Pixel Watch Community,
 
Safety Signal was originally introduced to provide Google Health Premium users LTE connectivity for essential safety features—such as Emergency SOS and Fall Detection—without requiring a standalone carrier plan for users with Google Health Premium.
 
This change applies to the following LTE-enabled models:
Google Pixel Watch 2 (LTE)
Google Pixel Watch 3 (LTE)
 
As we evolve our connectivity and safety capabilities, we will be ending Safety Signal on Dec 12.
 
What this means for Pixel Watch users:
Existing Safety Signal Users: Existing Safety Signal users will receive advance notice via email and push notifications. During the 30-day transition period from the date of the email, users may still access the features made available via Safety Signal without a standalone carrier plan. After  Dec 12, 2026,  this access will end. 
Other Safety Features: Core emergency features—such as Emergency SOS, Fall Detection, and Safety Check—will continue to work whenever your watch is connected to Wi-Fi, paired via Bluetooth with a phone nearby, or connected to an active cellular line through your carrier.
 
How to stay connected:
To maintain continuous access to Emergency SOS, Fall Detection, and cellular capabilities when away from your phone, you can set up a mobile plan or eSIM through a supported carrier. For step-by-step guidance, go to Manage your Google Pixel Watch with a cellular plan.
 
The Google Pixel Watch Team
