Rotate or flip video files online without installing any software. Supports MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI, MKV, WMV, FLV, and many other video formats, up to 200MB in size.

Upload a file from your device or paste a URL, choose rotation or flip options, and click the Rotate video! button. The tool outputs a new, permanently rotated video file - no watermarks, no registration required. You can choose to copy the original encoding for fastest processing, or re-encode as MP4 (h264/aac) or WebM (VP8/Vorbis) for better compatibility.

Common use cases include correcting smartphone videos that play sideways on desktop, flipping tutorial screen recordings for mirrored display, rotating videos filmed in portrait mode to landscape (or vice versa), and preparing clips for social media platforms that display content differently. The rotation is applied to every frame of the video, producing a clean, permanent result.
