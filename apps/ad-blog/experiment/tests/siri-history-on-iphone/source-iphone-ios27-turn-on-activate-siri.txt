Turn on and activate Siri on iPhone

Siri is an intelligent assistant that’s built into your iPhone to help you get answers, find what you need, and get things done.

Note: Siri is designed to protect your information and to let you choose what you share. To learn more, see the Siri, Dictation & Privacy website and the Improve Siri and Dictation & Privacy website.

Turn on Siri

Siri needs to be turned on before you can use it. If you didn’t set up Siri when you first set up your iPhone, do the following:

Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.

Tap Siri.

Tap Turn On Siri, then follow the onscreen instructions.

Tip: Tap Try Siri AI (Beta)* to get Siri AI, the new version of Siri powered by Apple Intelligence on iOS 27 or later. See Get started with Siri AI.

Activate Siri and make requests

After you turn on Siri, you need to activate it before you can ask a question or make a request.

Note: You must be connected to the internet to use Siri.

Activate Siri by doing one of the following:

Use your voice: Say “Siri” or “Hey Siri.” This option is only available if “Siri” or “Hey Siri” is turned on. See Customize how you activate Siri.

Use the buttons on iPhone: Press and hold the side button (on an iPhone with Face ID or iPhone Duo) or press and hold the Home button (on iPhone SE).

When you activate Siri with a button, Siri responds silently when iPhone is in Silent mode. When Silent mode is off, Siri responds out loud. When you activate Siri with your voice, Siri responds out loud. To change this behavior, see Change how Siri responds.

Use headphones connected to your iPhone: Press and hold the center or call button on your EarPods. For AirPods, see Set up Siri in the AirPods User Guide.

If you have Siri AI, you can also activate Siri by doing any of the following:

Use the Dynamic Island on iPhone: Swipe down from the top of the screen.

Use the shortcut menus on iPhone: Touch and hold something on your iPhone screen—a file or a photo, for example—then choose Ask Siri from the pop-up menu.

Use Siri mode in Camera on iPhone: Open Camera  on your iPhone, tap Siri, then tap , or click and hold the Camera Control. See Siri AI: Use Siri mode in Camera on iPhone.

Take a screenshot on iPhone: Quickly press and release the side button and the volume up button at the same time to take a screenshot, then tap  to ask a question,  to search, or tap an action that appears in the middle. See Siri AI: Learn about what’s on your iPhone screen.

Ask Siri a question or make a request.

See Get started with Siri AI and Use Siri to find information and get things done for ideas on how you can use Siri.

If Siri doesn’t work as expected on your iPhone, see the Apple Support article If Siri isn’t working on your iPhone or iPad.

Turn off Siri

Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.

Tap Siri.

Tap Turn Off Siri at the bottom, then tap Turn Off Siri.

If you have Siri AI but want to use the previous version of Siri, tap Turn On Siri, then tap Use Siri Classic.
