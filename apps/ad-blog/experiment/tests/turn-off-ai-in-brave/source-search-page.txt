Brave Search

The smarter way to search.
Get real answers. Faster.

AI-powered answers to almost any question, lightning fast and incredibly accurate. An independent index delivering actually useful results, without big tech’s SEO spam.

All in the same sleek results page.

Search without a trace. Search that puts you first.

Brave Search doesn’t profile you. It’s impossible for us to share, sell, or lose your personal data, because we don’t collect it in the first place.

Ask Brave

Get comprehensive answers to any question. Ask Brave combines the power of AI chat with the familiarity of a search results page.

Find useful summaries plus supporting videos, websites, products, & more, and then ask follow-up questions.

Independent

Even supposedly “neutral” or “private” search engines rely on big tech for results. Brave delivers results from its own, built-from-scratch index. No secret methods or algorithms, no bias or censorship.

Open to power the Web

Brave Search is available in any browser, at search.brave.com. Want to power your own search or AI app? Check out the Brave Search API.
