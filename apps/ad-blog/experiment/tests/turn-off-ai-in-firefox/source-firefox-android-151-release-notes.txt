Firefox for Android Release Notes
Release Notes tell you what's new in Firefox for Android. As always, we welcome your feedback. You can also file a bug in Bugzilla or see the system requirements of this release.
151.0
Firefox for Android Release
May 19, 2026
Version 151.0, first offered to Release channel users on May 19, 2026
Thanks for using Firefox! Have feedback or suggestions? We'd love to hear from you at Android Support

Firefox for Android 150.0
Next
Firefox for Android 152.0
New
AI controls: Keep Firefox AI features on your terms with one place to turn them on, off or customize them individually.
Gestures: Move between tabs faster by swiping vertically across the toolbar to see your open tabs.
Page summaries: Get quick, readable summaries of long articles with a tap or shake.
This feature is part of a progressive roll out.
What is a progressive roll out?
Certain new Firefox features are released gradually. This means some users will see the feature before everyone does. This approach helps to get early feedback to catch bugs and improve behavior quickly, meaning more Firefox users overall have a better experience.
Fixed
Various security fixes.
