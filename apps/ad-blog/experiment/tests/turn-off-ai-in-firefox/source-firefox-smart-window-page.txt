A different kind of privacy-first AI.On your terms.
Your chats stay private
Unlike most AI assistants, Mozilla doesn’t keep your chat transcripts, use your conversations to train models, or sell your data. To keep Smart Window running reliably, we process limited technical information. Your chat content is only shared when you choose.
You decide what it uses
“Memories” help Smart Window get more useful without compromising your privacy. They’re stored on your device, not on Mozilla’s servers, and we always try to filter out sensitive data. You can see what’s remembered, delete what doesn’t fit or turn it off.
