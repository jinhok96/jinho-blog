
Use a screen reader to manage Copilot settings in Windows
This article is for people who use a screen reader program such as Windows Narrator, JAWS, or NVDA with Windows tools or features and Microsoft 365 products. This article is part of the Accessibility help & learning content set where you can find more accessibility information on our apps. For general help, visit Microsoft Support.
Note
To learn more about screen readers, go to How screen readers work with Microsoft 365.
In Windows, you can configure Copilot launch preferences through Taskbar settings and adjust the functionality of the Copilot key on their keyboard. Keyboard shortcuts and screen readers may be utilized to activate Copilot and tailor its operation to individual requirements.
Note
Some options might not be available depending on your Microsoft 365 account type or organization settings. For more information, go to Welcome to the Microsoft Copilot app.

Turn on Copilot in Taskbar settings
Press Windows
 logo key +I to open Settings window.
Press Tab until you hear "System," then press the Down arrowkey until you hear "Personalization," and press Enter.
Press Tab to move to the options, then use the Down arrow key until you hear "Taskbar," and press Enter.
Press Tab until you hear “Ask Copilot toggle button,” and then press Spacebar to turn it on.
Once it’s turned on, press Windows
 logo key +C to open Copilot, or access Copilot from the taskbar.
Customize the Copilot key on your keyboard
Press Windows
 logo key +I to open Settings.
Press Tab until you hear "Bluetooth & devices," and press Enter.
Press Tab to move to the options, then use the Down arrow key until you hear "Keyboard," and press Enter.
Press Tab until you hear "Customise Copilot key on keyboard,” and then press Enter to open the list.
Use the Up or Down arrow key to move through the options like Search, Microsoft Copilot, Custom, and Copilot.
Note
The available options and default selection may differ based on your account settings and installed applications. If no option is selected, pressing, Windows
 logo key +C may not perform any action and could prompt you to configure the shortcut.
Select the desired option, and then press Enter.
Note
If Copilot is turned on in Taskbar settings, it overrides the option selected in Customise Copilot key on keyboard.
Available options may vary depending on your account type and installed apps.
