Clean up, extend, and reframe photos on iPhone

With Apple Intelligence, you can remove distracting objects, extend a photo beyond its original frame, and adjust its perspective.

Note: Apple Intelligence on iOS 27 or later is available on an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later. Apple Intelligence is not available in all languages or regions. Usage limits may apply.*

Clean up distractions

Use the Clean Up tool to remove distracting objects from a photo.

Note: Clean Up is not available in all languages or regions. For more information, see the Apple Support article Requirements to use Clean Up in Photos.

Go to the Photos app  on your iPhone.

Open a photo, then tap .

Tap Tools, then tap Clean Up.

Tap, brush, or circle what you want to remove.

Some items may be highlighted automatically so you can quickly tap to remove them.

You can use two fingers to pinch, zoom, or pan the image.

Tap  if you want to restore something you removed

Tap Clean Up (if you’re using High Quality processing).

Tap , then tap Done to close the photo.

To undo Clean Up changes after saving, follow steps 1 through 3 above, then tap Reset.

You can choose the processing type Photos uses for Clean Up—Auto, Fast, or High Quality. Tap Auto during the Clean Up process, then choose an option.

Extend the background

Use the Extend tool to expand the edges of the original photo frame.

Go to the Photos app  on your iPhone.

Open a photo, then tap .

Tap Tools, then tap Extend.

Pinch or drag the photo to extend the background; drag the edge of the frame to crop.

Tap Extend.

Tap , then tap Done to close the photo.

After you save, you can undo the changes you made with the Extend tool. Follow steps 1 through 3 above, then tap Reset.

Reframe the perspective

Use the Reframe tool to change the point of capture after you take a photo.

Go to the Photos app  on your iPhone.

Open a photo, then tap .

Tap Tools, then tap Reframe.

Touch and drag the photo to adjust the perspective or use two fingers to pan, zoom, and rotate.

Tap Reframe.

Tap the photo to compare the reframed version to the original.

Tap , then tap Done to close the photo.

After you save, you can undo the changes you made with the Reframe tool. Follow steps 1 through 3 above, then tap Reset.

In iOS 27.2 or later, most images created or edited with Apple Intelligence tools include a SynthID hidden watermark to help identify whether an image was generated or modified with generative tools. You can use the SynthID Detector to check for this watermark.
