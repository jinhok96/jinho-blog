Create original images with Image Playground on iPhone

In the Image Playground app, you can use Apple Intelligence to create original images within seconds. Get started using simple text descriptions, photos, and people from your Photos library. You can also use Image Playground to create images in Messages, Lock Screen wallpapers, contact posters, and more.

Note: Apple Intelligence on iOS 27 or later is available on an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later. Apple Intelligence is not available in all languages or regions. Usage limits may apply.*

Create an image

Go to the Image Playground app  on your iPhone.

Tap .

Do any of the following to customize your image:

Describe it: Enter a description.

Choose a style: Tap the Style button, then choose an Apple Intelligence style (like Any Style or Illustration) or tap ChatGPT** to describe the style you want.

Note: If you choose Any Style, you can describe the style you want as part of your image description. If you choose Genmoji, you can save it with your stickers.

Add a person from your Photos library: Tap , then select a person. You can add up to three people.

Note: If you provide a text description that doesn’t match the person you selected, your image is personalized based on the photo you selected rather than the text description.

A photo of something else, like a pet, nature, or food: Tap , then choose an option.

Choose the aspect ratio: Tap , then choose an option.

Tap  to create your image.

All changes are automatically saved to your gallery. If you created a Genmoji, tap Add Emoji to add it with your stickers. To view the images in your gallery, tap . All the versions you create for a single idea are grouped together.

In addition to using the Image Playground app, you can also use Image Playground features to create original images in apps like Freeform and Messages. See Create and send original images using Image Playground in Messages and Create new, original images in Freeform. Image Playground is also available in Keynote, Pages, Apple Invites, supported third-party apps, and more.

Edit an existing image

Go to the Image Playground app  on your iPhone.

Tap an image in the gallery. You may have to tap  to see your gallery.

Do any of the following to refine your image:

Describe a change: Enter a description to transform the background, change the style, or add something entirely new.

Make targeted edits: Tap , then tap, brush, or circle a specific object. You can move it, resize it, or swap it out with another image. Use the buttons at the bottom of the screen to add elements, or to change or remove the object you selected. Tap Apply Change when you’re ready to save your edits.

Note: Swipe up or down to move between versions of your image. You can move to a different version at any time and keep refining from there.

When you’re finished, tap .

Share, copy, or delete an image

Go to the Image Playground app  on your iPhone.

Tap an image, then do any of the following:

Share your image: Tap , then choose a share option.

Copy your image: Tap , then tap Copy or Duplicate.

Delete your image: Tap , then tap Delete.

Note: If you delete an image from Image Playground, it’s deleted on all your devices where you’re signed in to the same Apple Account.

In iOS 27.2 or later, most images created or edited with Apple Intelligence tools include a SynthID hidden watermark to help identify whether an image was generated or modified with generative tools. You can use the SynthID Detector to check for this watermark.

You can block access to image creation features, like Image Playground, in Screen Time settings. See Block or allow Siri and Apple Intelligence features. The recommended age for realistic images is 18+. For more information on parental controls, see Apple Devices for Kids and Families.
