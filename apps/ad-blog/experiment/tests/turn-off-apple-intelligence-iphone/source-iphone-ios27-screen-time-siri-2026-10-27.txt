Block features or content with Screen Time on iPhone

You can allow or block content that’s labeled sensitive or explicit in the Music app, Apple TV app, Books app, and more. You can also allow or block built in apps and features like AirDrop, Camera, and more.

Note: To use all the latest Screen Time features, you need iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, or later on all your Apple devices. If you’re setting up these features in a Family Sharing group, all devices must be upgraded to iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, or later. Learn how to update your Apple devices.

	

If you want to set up Screen Time or parental control features for your child, see Apple Devices for Kids and Families.

Set content restrictions in the Music app, Apple TV app, Books app, and more

Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.

Tap Screen Time, tap Content & Privacy Restrictions, then allow or block any of the settings shown under Content Restrictions, like Books, Movies, TV Shows, and Music.

Block or allow built-in apps or features

Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.

Tap Screen Time, tap Content & Privacy Restrictions, tap Other Features, then allow or block any of the settings shown, like AirDrop, Camera, and more.

Block or allow Siri and Apple Intelligence features

Note: The settings that appear here may vary depending on your Siri version. Siri AI is available in beta on iOS 27 or later on an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later with Siri and device language set to a supported language. Available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish to start. Siri AI will not initially be available in the EU on iOS. Apple Intelligence and Siri AI are not available in China mainland. Siri AI is not available for users under 13. Usage limits may apply. Apple Intelligence on iOS 27 or later is available on an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later. Apple Intelligence is not available in all languages or regions. Usage limits may apply.

Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.

Tap Screen Time, tap Content & Privacy Restrictions, tap Siri, then do any of the following:

Block Siri: Tap Don’t Allow Siri.

Allow use of Siri without AI capabilities: Tap Siri (Classic Version).

Allow use of Siri with AI capabilities: Tap Siri AI (Beta).*

Allow or block specific capabilities: Select Allow or Block under any of the Siri AI capabilities, such as Image Creation, Sensitive Topics, or Explicit Language.

*Apple Intelligence is available with Siri and device language set to Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, or Vietnamese. Some features may not be available in all regions or languages. Some devices may not be available in all regions. Siri AI is available in beta in iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27 and requires an Apple Intelligence–enabled device set to a supported language. Available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish to start. Siri AI will not be initially available in the EU on iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS. Siri AI is not available for users under 13. Certain Apple Intelligence features that rely on server-side models are subject to daily usage limits, including but not limited to Siri AI, Intelligent Photo Editing Tools, Image Playground, and AFM 3 Cloud models in Shortcuts. Daily limits may vary by feature, request complexity, system demand, system policies, and other factors. Expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future. Use of these features is subject to the Apple Intelligence Terms and Conditions. Learn more at support.apple.com/148587.
