What's changing with Copilot?

We’re updating Copilot to create a simpler, more cohesive experience for everyone. Depending on the account and device you use, you will see changes to the Copilot app including changes to appearance and functionality, such as navigation, feature availability, or sign-in experience. You will be able to sign in to the updated Copilot app with a personal account, a work or school account, or both. Work and personal accounts remain separate. Your organization’s security, privacy, compliance, and administrative controls continue to apply when you use Copilot with your work or school account.

What improvements will the updated Copilot app have?
Will I need to pay to use the Copilot app?

When you hit a limit, you can choose to upgrade to a paid option or come back later. Copilot users in Edge or using the Copilot Windows app may benefit from a higher free usage allowance. 

For higher limits to chat and create, use agents, or tackle complex multi-step tasks, purchase a Microsoft 365 subscription.

Will data be shared between my personal and work accounts?
Can I switch between my personal email and work or school (Microsoft Entra ID) accounts?

Yes. You can continue to switch between personal and work or school accounts using the account switcher within Copilot, just as you might be doing today. Work and personal experiences remain separated by design, and data does not flow between them.

Is anything going away as part of this update?

Most user data will be moved with you to the updated Copilot app: your chats, images and other content you’ve created with Copilot will be migrated. Group Chat threads, messages, and content, including images created in Group Chats, will not be carried forward. Podcasts will also not be carried forward. Before your account updates, copy any messages you want to keep into a document or notes app and download any images you want to save.  

Some features may be temporarily unavailable, or you may experience functionality gaps while the latest version of Copilot is being rolled out. Learn more.
