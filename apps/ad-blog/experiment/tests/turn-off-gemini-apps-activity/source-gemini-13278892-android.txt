Manage & delete your activity in Gemini Apps

When you are signed in and Keep Activity is on, Google stores your Gemini Apps activity in your Google Account. You can review your prompts, delete your Gemini Apps activity, and turn off Keep Activity in My Activity at any time.

For more info about your Gemini Apps data and how you can manage it, go to the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub.

Important:

To manage Gemini Apps Activity, you need to sign in to Gemini Apps. Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps.
This article covers Gemini Apps activity for a personal account. If you use Gemini Apps with a work or school account, your Gemini Apps Activity settings are controlled by your Google Workspace administrator. You may not be able to change your retention settings or see how long your chats are retained for Learn more about Gemini Apps activity for work and school users.

Find your Gemini Apps activity
In the Gemini mobile app
On your Android phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app .
At the left, tap Menu   your Profile picture or initial  Gemini Apps Activity.
In the Gemini web app
On your Android phone or tablet, go to gemini.google.com.
At the top, tap Menu   Settings & help   Activity .
You can also go to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
Go to Gemini Apps Activity
Tips to find specific Gemini Apps activity
For conversations with Gems, the Gem used is noted in your Gemini Apps activity.
For Gemini Live chats, the transcripts, audio, files, images, & YouTube videos, and any video or screenshares you choose to share with Live are saved in Gemini Apps Activity if your Keep Activity setting is on. 
If activity includes specific data from your Live chat, it shows a related icon below it.
An audio icon  means it includes audio.
A screen sharing icon  means it includes video frames of your shared screen.
A video icon  means it includes video frames of your camera stream.
To view or download this audio, screenshare, or video data, select Details.
Delete your activity

In the Gemini mobile app
On your Android phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app .
At the left, tap Menu   your Profile picture or initial  Gemini Apps Activity.
Choose the Gemini Apps activity you want to delete. You can choose:
All your Gemini Apps activity: Above your activity, tap Delete   All time.
The last hour or day: Above your activity, tap Delete   Last hour or Last day.
A specific time period: Above your activity, tap Delete   Custom range.
A specific day: Next to the day, tap Delete all activity from [day] .
A specific activity: Next to the activity, tap Delete activity item .
In the Gemini web app
On your Android phone or tablet, go to gemini.google.com.
At the top, tap Menu   Settings & help   Activity .
You can also go to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
Choose the Gemini Apps activity you want to delete. You can choose:
All your Gemini Apps activity: Above your activity, tap Delete   All time.
The last hour or day: Above your activity, tap Delete   Last hour or Last day.
A specific time period: Above your activity, tap Delete   Custom range.
A specific day: Next to the day, tap Delete all activity from [day] .
A specific activity: Next to the activity, tap Delete activity item .

Tip: If you use Gemini in Google Messages, learn how to delete your related chat.

What happens when Gemini Apps activity is deleted
If a conversation with a Gem is deleted, it doesn’t delete the Gem. Learn how to delete your Gems.
If a shared chat is deleted, it doesn’t delete the related public link. Learn how to delete a public link.
If you delete activity for a Gemini Live chat, any related audio, transcript, videos, and screenshares for that activity are also deleted.
Turn off Keep Activity

Important:

If you’re 18 or over, Keep Activity is on by default.
If Keep Activity is off, any Gems you create will still be saved to your account. Any chats you create with Gems will not be saved. Learn more about what happens with your data when you use Gems.

In the Gemini mobile app
On your Android phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app .
At the left, tap Menu   your Profile picture or initial  Gemini Apps Activity.
Near the top, tap On  Turn off or Turn off and delete activity.
Even when Keep Activity is off, your conversations will be saved with your account for up to 72 hours to allow Google to provide the service and process any feedback. This activity won’t appear in your Gemini Apps Activity.
In the Gemini web app

To turn off Keep Activity:

On your Android phone or tablet, go to gemini.google.com.
At the top, tap Menu   Settings & help   Activity .
Near the top, tap On  Turn off or Turn off and delete activity.
Even when Keep Activity is off, your conversations will be saved with your account for up to 72 hours to allow Google to provide the service and process any feedback. This activity won’t appear in your Gemini Apps Activity.
When Keep Activity is off

If Keep Activity is turned off, only the following apps are available:

Device assistance

Manage whether your audio & Gemini Live recordings are used to improve Google services

Important:

This setting is off by default.
To turn on this setting, you must have Keep Activity on.

You can let Google use your audio and Gemini Live videos and screenshares to improve and develop its services, including training generative AI models. Learn more about this setting.

To turn on this setting:

In the Gemini mobile app
At the left, tap Menu   your Profile picture or initial  Gemini Apps Activity.
If Gemini App Activity is off, tap Off. Then review the disclosures and tap Turn on.
Next to Improve Google services with your audio and Gemini Live videos & screenshares, select the box.
Tap Turn on.
In the Gemini web app
At the left, tap Menu   Settings & help   Activity .
If Gemini App Activity is off, tap Off. Then review the disclosures and tap Turn on.
Next to Improve Google services with your audio and Gemini Live videos & screenshares, select the box.
Tap Turn on.
Tip: You can turn off this setting at any time.
Change Auto-delete settings

In the Gemini mobile app
On your Android phone or tablet, open the Gemini mobile app .
At the left, tap Menu   your Profile picture or initial  Gemini Apps Activity.
Near the top, tap Deleting activity older than … months or Choose an auto-delete option.
Choose how long your Gemini Apps activity is kept (3, 18, or 36 months) or select Don’t auto-delete activity.
Tap Next and follow the on-screen instructions.
In the Gemini web app

By default, your Gemini Apps activity older than 18 months is auto-deleted. You can turn off Auto-delete or change the auto-delete period to 3 or 36 months.

On your Android phone or tablet, go to gemini.google.com.
At the top, tap Menu   Settings & help   Activity .
Near the top, tap Deleting activity older than … months or Choose an auto-delete option.
Choose how long your Gemini Apps activity is kept (3, 18, or 36 months) or select Don’t auto-delete activity.
Tap Next and follow the on-screen instructions.
How your Gemini Apps activity is deleted from your Google Account

When you choose to delete activity manually or activity is deleted automatically, we immediately start the process of removing it from the product and our systems.

First, we aim to immediately remove it from view. Then, we begin a process designed to safely and completely delete the data from our storage systems.

Learn more about how Google helps you delete data from your account.
