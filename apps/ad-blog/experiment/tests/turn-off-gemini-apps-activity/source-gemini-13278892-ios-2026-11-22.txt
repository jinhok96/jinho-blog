Manage & delete your history in Gemini Apps

When you are signed in and History is on, Google stores your Gemini history in your Google Account. You can review your prompts, delete your Gemini history, and turn off the History setting at any time.

For more info about your Gemini Apps data and how you can manage it, go to the Gemini Apps Privacy Hub.

Important:

To manage Gemini History, you need to sign in to Gemini Apps. Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps.
This article covers Gemini history for a personal account. If you use Gemini Apps with a work or school account, your Gemini History settings are controlled by your Google Workspace administrator. You may not be able to change your retention settings or see how long your chats are retained for. Learn more about Gemini History for work and school users.

Tips to find specific Gemini history
If history includes specific data from a Gemini Live chat, it shows a related icon below it.
An audio icon  means it includes audio.
A screen sharing icon  means it includes video frames of your shared screen.
A video icon  means it includes video frames of your camera stream.
To view or download this audio, screenshare, or video data, select Details.
Delete your history
In the Gemini mobile app
On your iPhone or iPad, open the Gemini mobile app .
At the  left, tap Menu   your Profile picture or initial  Gemini History.
Choose the Gemini history you want to delete. You can choose:
All your Gemini history Above your history, tap Delete   All time.
The last hour or day: Above your history tap Delete   Last hour or Last day.
A specific time period: Above your history, tap Delete   Custom range.
A specific day: Next to the day, tap Delete all history from [day] .
A specific history item: Next to the history item tap Delete history item .
In the Gemini web app
On your iPhone or iPad, go to gemini.google.com.
At the top, tap Menu   Settings   History .
You can also go to myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
Choose the Gemini history you want to delete. You can choose:
All your Gemini history Above your history, tap Delete   All time.
The last hour or day: Above your history tap Delete   Last hour or Last day.
A specific time period: Above your history, tap Delete   Custom range.
A specific day: Next to the day, tap Delete all history from [day] .
A specific history item: Next to the history item tap Delete history item .
What happens when Gemini history is deleted
If a shared chat is deleted, it doesn’t delete the related public link. Learn how to delete a public link.
If you delete history for a Gemini Live chat, any related audio, transcript, videos, and screenshares for that history are also deleted.
Turn off History

Important: If you’re 18 or over, History is on by default.

In the Gemini mobile app
On your iPhone or iPad, open the Gemini mobile app .
At the  left, tap Menu   your Profile picture or initial  Gemini History.
Near the top, tap On  Turn off or Turn off and delete history.
Even when History is off, your conversations will be saved with your account for around two months to allow Google to provide the service, process any feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public. This history won’t appear in your Gemini History.
In the Gemini web app

To turn off History:

On your iPhone or iPad, go to gemini.google.com.
At the top, tap Menu   Settings   History .
Near the top, tap On  Turn off or Turn off and delete history.
Even when History is off, your conversations will be saved with your account for around two months to allow Google to provide the service, process any feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public. This history won’t appear in your Gemini History.

Manage whether your chat content and audio & Gemini Live recordings are used to train generative AI models

Important:

To turn on these settings, you must have History on.
You can turn these settings on or off at any time
"Chat content including text & attachments" is on by default if you are over 18.
"Audio and Gemini Live videos & screenshares" is off by default
To turn these settings on or off:
You control whether Google uses your chat content, including text & attachments, to train generative AI models. Learn more about this setting.

You can also let Google use your audio and Gemini Live videos & screenshares to improve and develop its services, including training generative AI models. Learn more about this setting.

In the Gemini mobile app
At the top, tap Menu  .
At the bottom, tap Settings .  
Under "Data & privacy" tap History .
Select which setting or settings you want to turn on or off.
Next to “Chat content including text & uploads”, check or uncheck the box.
Next to "Audio and Gemini Live videos & screenshares", check or uncheck the box.
Tap Turn on or Turn off.

Tip:  If you aren't able to check either box, you may need to turn History on.

In the Gemini web app
At the top, tap Menu  .
At the bottom, tap Settings   History .
Select which setting or settings you want to turn on or off.
Next to “Chat content including text & uploads”, check or uncheck the box.
Next to "Audio and Gemini Live videos & screenshares", check or uncheck the box.
Tap Turn on or Turn off.

Tip:  If you aren't able to check either box, you may need to turn History on.

 
Change Auto-delete settings
In the Gemini mobile app

Gemini history older than 18 months is auto-deleted by default. You can turn off auto-delete or change the auto-delete period to 3 or 36 months.

On your iPhone or iPad, open the Gemini mobile app .
At the  left, tap Menu   your Profile picture or initial  Gemini History.
Near the top, tap Deleting history older than … months or Choose an auto-delete option.
Choose how long your Gemini history is kept (3, 18, or 36 months) or select Don’t auto-delete history.
Tap Next and follow the on-screen instructions.
In the Gemini web app

Gemini history older than 18 months is auto-deleted by default. You can turn off auto-delete or change the auto-delete period to 3 or 36 months.

On your iPhone or iPad, go to gemini.google.com.
At the top, tap Menu   Settings   History .
Near the top, tap Deleting history older than … months or Choose an auto-delete option.
Choose how long your Gemini history is kept (3, 18, or 36 months) or select Don’t auto-delete history.
Tap Next and follow the on-screen instructions.
How your Gemini history is deleted from your Google Account

When you choose to delete history manually or history is deleted automatically, we immediately start the process of removing it from the product and our systems.
