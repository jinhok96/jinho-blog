Get personalization with memory of your past Gemini chats

You can get a personalized experience from Gemini Apps using memory of your past chats. With this feature, Gemini can learn from your chats to understand more about you and your world.

What you need

To get personalization with memory of your past Gemini chats, you need to:

Be 18 or over.
Sign in with a personal Google Account. This feature isn’t available when you sign in to a work, school, or supervised Google Account. Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps.
Have Keep Activity on. This feature isn’t available when this setting is off.

Important: For now, this feature is:

Only available in the Gemini mobile app, the Gemini web app at gemini.google.com, Gemini in Chrome (in countries where Gemini in Chrome is available), and Gemini on your smartwatch.
Not available in certain features, like Gems or Live chats. In a text chat, you can ask Gemini to reference a past Gemini Live conversation.

Turn Memory on or off

You can change whether Gemini Apps use memory of your past chats to personalize your experience at any time.

On your computer, go to gemini.google.com.
At the bottom, click Settings & help   Personal Intelligence.
Turn Memory off  or on .
Tip: When Memory is turned on, Gemini may suggest topics or tools for you based on previous conversations. For instance, Gemini might:
Suggest next steps for a project you’re working on
Offer to help plan a trip you’ve mentioned
Ask if you want help comparing products you’ve discussed.
Find & delete your past Gemini chats

You can find and delete your past chats in Gemini Apps Activity. If you delete a chat, there might be a short delay before Gemini stops using it to personalize your responses. Learn how to manage your Gemini Apps activity.

Check if Gemini used your past chats

To check if Gemini used your past chats, just ask Gemini, “Did you use any info from past chats?”

Delete or correct something Gemini knows about you

To delete something that Gemini remembered about you, delete all chats with this info from Gemini Apps Activity. Learn how to manage your Gemini Apps.

To correct what Gemini knows about you and uses in a response:

Make sure that Memory is turned on. Learn how to turn Memory on or off.
Correct Gemini directly in your chat.

While Gemini continues to improve, it might not always get it right.

Delete something Gemini knows about you from a connected app

If you want to delete something that Gemini remembered about you:

Delete all chats with this info from Gemini Apps Activity or Recent chats. Learn how to manage your Gemini Apps Activity and delete a chat from Recent chats.
Disconnect the app with this info, so Gemini doesn’t use it again. Learn how to manage your Connected Apps.

You must complete both steps because:

If you only disconnect the app, Gemini might still use the info if it’s in a past chat.
If you only delete the chats, Gemini might still find the info in the connected app.
What happens when you delete data from a connected app

If you delete or update data in a connected app, like an email from Gmail or photo from Google Photos, your experience in Gemini may not be affected until days later.
