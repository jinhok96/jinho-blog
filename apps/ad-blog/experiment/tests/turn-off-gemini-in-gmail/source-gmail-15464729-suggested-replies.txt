## Use Suggested Replies in Gmail
If you’re short on time or need to find the right words, Gemini in Gmail can review an email that you receive and suggest a relevant response. With Suggested Replies, you get a personalized message that matches the tone and style of your past written emails.
## What you need
Important: Even if you meet the requirements, a suggested reply may not always be available.
To get a suggested reply, make sure:
- Your Google Account is eligible. You can select a suggested reply if you have:
- A personal Google Account in the US.
- An eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. Learn more about Gemini features and plans.
- Workspace smart features are on in your Gmail settings. Learn more about smart features.
- Your Google Account language is set to English. Learn how to change your language.
Suggested Replies are also available if your account is enrolled in Google Workspace Experiments, which is a trusted tester program for users to try new AI features. Learn how to get started with Google Workspace Experiments.
## Select a suggested reply
- On your Android phone or tablet, open the Gmail app .
- Open an email.
- At the bottom of the email, tap a suggested reply.
- Before you send a response, you can edit it.
- Tap Send .
## Learn how Gmail personalizes Suggested Replies
Important:
- Suggested Replies with personalization are available in English only.
- Personalized responses require an eligible Google Workspace plan, a Google AI plan, or enrollment in Google Workspace Experiments.
- To get personalized responses, you need an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. Personalization is not available for personal Google Accounts without a Google AI plan. Learn about Gemini features and plans.
To improve your response, Suggested Replies use details from your emails, such as flight times, soccer schedules, or hotel booking codes. Gmail drafts a personalized response with relevant details and matches your tone and style.
Tip: To find the emails Gemini uses, select Sources above a suggestion. You can't edit the list of sources.
## Give feedback about a suggested reply
Google Workspace with Gemini is constantly learning and may not be able to support your request.
If you get a suggestion that’s inaccurate or that you feel is unsafe, you can submit feedback.
- At the bottom of the compose window, swipe right on the suggested replies.
- Tap Send feedback .
- Follow the on-screen instructions.
Important:
- When you give us feedback, you can choose to include your device logs with the request. These logs help Google when you report a technical issue. Learn how to manage device logs on Android.
- To send feedback with screenshots, tap More  in the top right of the window  Help & feedback  Send feedback. Learn more about sending feedback in Gmail.
- To report a legal issue, create a request.
## Learn about Gemini feature suggestions
- Gemini feature suggestions don’t represent Google’s views, and should not be attributed to Google.
- Don’t rely on Gemini features as medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.
- Gemini features may suggest inaccurate or inappropriate information. Your feedback makes Gemini more helpful and safe.
- Enterprise end users can submit feedback about their experience using this feature. End users are informed before they submit feedback that the feedback data shouldn’t contain personal, sensitive, or confidential information.
