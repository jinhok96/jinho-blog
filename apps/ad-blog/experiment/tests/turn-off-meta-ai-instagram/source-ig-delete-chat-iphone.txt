You can remove conversations you see in your inbox on Instagram by deleting them. Once you delete a conversation, it will no longer be visible in your inbox.
Delete a chat in your inbox
Tap Messages at the bottom.

Tap and hold the conversation you'd like to delete.
Tap Delete, then tap Delete to confirm.
Keep in mind
Deleting a conversation in your inbox will only delete it for you, and that it will still appear in the inbox of other people that are part of the conversation.
Your Data Download file doesn't include messages that you've deleted from your Instagram account.
