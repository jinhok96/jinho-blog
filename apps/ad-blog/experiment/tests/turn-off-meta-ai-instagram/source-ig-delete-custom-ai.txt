Custom AIs on Instagram aren’t yet available for everyone.
You can delete an custom AI you created on Instagram at any time.
Delete your custom AI
Go to aistudio.instagram.com using a computer.
Click Your AIs.
Click  Options next to the AI you want to delete.
Click Delete AI.

When you delete a custom AI that someone has chatted with, they’ll be able to see their history with it. They won’t be able to send that AI any new messages.
People who create an AI in Meta AI Studio do not have access to the chats other people have with that AI, unless they’re a creator using an AI based on themselves to respond to their personal messages. Learn more about AI auto replies.
