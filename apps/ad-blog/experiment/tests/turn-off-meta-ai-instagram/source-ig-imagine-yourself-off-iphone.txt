This feature is in development and being gradually introduced in the United States. It may not be available to you yet.
Meta AI may generate images of you once you've set up the feature, but you can still turn this feature off at any time in Meta AI settings. Turning this off will delete your setup photos and you will need to retake your photos if you turn this feature back on.
Turn off AI images of you on Instagram
Open your chat with Meta AI.
Tap  AI info.
Tap Imagine yourself.
Tap  Toggle next to Create AI images of yourself with Meta AI to turn off this feature and delete your photos.

Retake your setup photos for AI images of you
Tap the Meta AI tab to open the chat with Meta AI.
Tap  AI info.
Tap Imagine yourself.
Tap Retake your photos, then follow the on-screen prompts to retake your photos.
You may also see AI images of you appear in Feed. Only you can see these images unless you share them, but you can also turn this off.
Turn off AI images of you in Feed
Open your chat with Meta AI.
Tap  AI info.
Tap Imagine yourself.
Tap  Toggle next to See AI images of yourself in Feed to stop showing AI images of yourself in Feed.
Replace your uploaded photos for AI-generated images of you
Tap the Meta AI tab to open the chat with Meta AI.
Tap  AI info.
Tap Imagine yourself.
Tap Uploaded photos of yourself, then follow the on-screen prompts to replace your photos.

Get help with turning off or retaking setup photos for AI images of you on Instagram
You can manage AI images of yourself on Instagram through the Meta AI settings. From the Imagine yourself menu in your Meta AI chat, you can turn off the feature to delete your setup photos, retake your photos, or replace specific uploaded images. You can also choose to stop AI images of yourself from appearing in your Feed.
