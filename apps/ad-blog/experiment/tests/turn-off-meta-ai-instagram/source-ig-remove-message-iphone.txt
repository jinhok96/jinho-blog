You can remove a message that appears in your Instagram chat by deleting it.
Note: Deleting messages in your conversation does not delete them for others in the chat. You can instead, unsend a message you’ve sent.
Delete a message
Tap Messages at the bottom.

Tap a conversation.
Tap and hold a message you’ve sent or received.
Tap Delete for you.
Tap  Select next to each message you want to delete.
Tap Delete for you at the bottom and confirm your choice.
Keep in mind
When you download a copy of your information, your data download file won’t include messages that you've deleted.
If the chat or a surrounding message is reported, deleted messages could also be included in the report.

Get help with removing a message in your Instagram chat
You can remove messages from your Instagram chat by tapping and holding a message and selecting Delete for you. This action only removes the message for you and does not delete it for other people in the conversation.
