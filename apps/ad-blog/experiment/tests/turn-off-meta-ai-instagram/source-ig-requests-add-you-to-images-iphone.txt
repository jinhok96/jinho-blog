You may receive requests from friends on Instagram to add you to images and vibes they create on the Meta AI app. You can make AI generated images or videos (called vibes) of yourself or other people on the Meta AI app.
To get started creating AI images and videos including people:
Add yourself to images and videos on Meta AI: Take and upload photos of yourself to create AI images and vibes of yourself. You can also let others add you to images and vibes.
Ask friends to create images and vibes of them: You can request permission to add your friends to images or vibes. You may be able to make images and videos of mutual friends already, depending on their settings.
Learn more about creating AI generated images and videos of yourself or others on the Meta AI app.
