You can use Meta AI on Instagram to help write or rephrase a message before it’s sent in a chat with others. For example, you can use Meta AI to change the tone or length of your message. You can edit any AI-generated text before sending it.
Write with Meta AI
In an existing conversation, tap Message… at the bottom.
Enter your message and then tap  Edit with Meta AI next to your message. Note: Be mindful before including any personal information in your message (examples: name, address, email, or phone number).
Meta AI will appear. In the text box, enter a prompt to describe the changes you’d like to make to your message (example: make funnier) and then tap  Send next to your request to send it to Meta AI. Note: You can also tap a suggested prompt for Meta AI above the text box.
Meta AI will generate a new message from your prompt. From here, you can:
Generate another message using the same prompt by tapping  Reload next to your requested change.
Tap your prompt to enter a different change for Meta AI to use for writing your message.
Submit feedback for Meta AI by tapping and holding the message Meta AI shared, then tapping Good result or Bad result.
When you’re ready, tap Apply in the top right to send your message in the chat.
Before sending a message in a chat, you can also tap and hold the text to highlight a certain part of the message and then tap Write with AI to rewrite only that part.
Note that AI text generated automatically in response to your prompt may be inaccurate or inappropriate.
