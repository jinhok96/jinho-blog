About Meta AI
Copy link
Accounts Center is being renamed to Meta Account. We are gradually rolling out this update, and you may see either Accounts Center or Meta Account as the place to manage your settings across apps. Learn more about Meta Accounts.
Adding WhatsApp to your Meta Account is optional. WhatsApp will only be in your Meta Account if you previously added it to the same Accounts Center or if you choose to add it to your Meta Account when it becomes available.
Meta AI through WhatsApp is an optional service from Meta that can answer your questions, teach you something, or help come up with new ideas.
Meta provides the generative AI technology that powers AI experiences available through WhatsApp. Learn about AI experiences available through WhatsApp in this article.
Note:
Meta AI and some experiences are currently only available in limited countries and might not be available to you yet, even if others in your country have access.
You can chat with Meta AI in any language. However, in some markets, access to Meta AI is only available for devices in certain languages. We're continuing to expand access. Learn more in this article.
You can:
Have conversations with Meta AI
Chat with Meta AI in existing group chats
Ask questions and get helpful recommendations
Talk about shared interests
Interact with their content
Create AI-generated images in chats
Forward messages to Meta AI to ask questions or learn more
As we continue to test new ways to improve interactions with Meta AI, some users might see different options to access Meta AI through WhatsApp. Some users may see a pink and purple oval circle  or a blue and purple circle  on the Chats tab.
Feature availability varies by region. When you chat with Meta AI, messages from Meta AI are generated by artificial intelligence (AI), using a service from Meta, in response to the prompts and messages you send to the AI. Learn how Meta uses information for generative AI models in this article. To learn more about AI chats and privacy on WhatsApp, read more here.
When you choose to use these features, Meta receives your prompts, the messages you share, and feedback to deliver relevant responses directly to you.
Only messages that mention @Meta AI, or that people choose to share with Meta AI, can be read by Meta. Meta can’t read any other messages in your personal chats. When you share your personal messages for AI features that use Private Processing technology, Meta can’t read or access the messages you’ve shared.‬ Learn more in this article.
As always, your personal messages and calls are protected with end-to-end encryption, meaning no one outside of the chat, not even WhatsApp or Meta, can read, listen to, or share them.
Note:
Remember that Meta AI isn’t a human, even though it may chat and respond in ways that seem like a real person. Be mindful of how much time you spend chatting with Meta AI, and take a break when you need to.
Meta may use your prompts, the messages you share, and feedback to improve AI quality.
You have the option to delete individual chats with Meta AI or request the deletion of information you previously shared with Meta AI. Learn more in this article.
Meta uses AI chats consistent with Meta’s Privacy Policy. By using this feature, you agree to Meta’s AI Terms of Service.
Meta may receive information about your device, your connection, and how you use Meta AI to provide Meta AI experiences and product improvements.
Some messages generated by Meta AI might not be accurate or appropriate.
In some countries, to provide you with more relevant responses, Meta AI may use information from across accounts you’ve added to your Meta Account, such as Facebook or Instagram, and things like your interests, age, location, and profiles. This information may also be used to improve AI at Meta. You can see how Meta uses this information in the Meta Privacy Center. Learn more in this article.
Meta AI may use sources and links from the internet to inform its responses. You have the option to view and report a source or link. You can find more information in this article.
