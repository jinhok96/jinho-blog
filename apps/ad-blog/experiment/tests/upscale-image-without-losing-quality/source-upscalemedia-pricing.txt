Have questions? We’re Here to Help

Here are answers to the most common questions about Upscale.media and our pricing. If you need help with anything else, feel free to reach out via chat or email us at sales@pixelbin.io.

What are credits

Credits are used to measure how much you use the platform. Each image upscale generally uses 1 credit, though this may vary by complexity of the transformation.

Do I need to sign up for upscaling images?

Sign-up is required for bulk transformations and full access to the console. Without signing up, you can upscale one image per day for free.

Is Upscale.media free?

You get 3 free credits when you sign up, no payment required. You can use these to try out the platform. For regular or bulk use, you can switch to a subscription or pay-as-you-go plan.

Should I use pay-as-you-go credits or a subscription plan?

Use pay-as-you-go if you only need to process a few images occasionally. For ongoing or bulk use, a subscription plan offers better value.

What if I exhaust my credit limit?

You can top up your account anytime using pay-as-you-go credits, or upgrade to a higher subscription plan based on your needs.

What happens to unused credits at the end of the month?

Unused monthly credits expire at the end of each billing cycle. If you want more flexibility, pay-as-you-go credits are also available.

Can I cancel, upgrade, or downgrade my subscription?

Yes. You can change your plan from the pricing section or reach out to support for assistance. For custom plans, we’re happy to schedule a consultation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards. For payment-related help, contact our team at support@pixelbin.io.

What kind of transformations are supported?

You can upscale images, remove or change backgrounds, and apply custom presets for bulk processing. Advanced editing is available through the Pixelbin console.

Can I perform multiple edits in one go?

Yes. You can combine transformations like background removal and upscaling by creating presets through Pixelbin. These can be applied in bulk via the dashboard.

Is Upscale.media suitable for design or creative work?

Absolutely. It works well for design projects, digital art, marketing assets, and other visual content where high-resolution output is essential.

How accurate is the upscaling?

The platform delivers fast and high-quality results, powered by advanced image enhancement methods. If you're ever not satisfied, our support team is here to help.

Will I be charged automatically when my free credits run out?

No, you will not be charged automatically when your free credits are exhausted. Upscale.media only processes payments if you choose to purchase additional credits or subscribe to a plan. You remain in control and can decide when, and if, you want to upgrade.

Do you offer custom plans or special features?

Yes. If you have unique requirements, contact us and we’ll help you find a solution that fits your use case.
